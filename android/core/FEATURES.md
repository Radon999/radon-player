# Radon Player

**All your music in one place, on your phone and your computer.**

Radon Player plays your own music files and the streaming services you use, together, in FLAC (hi-res where it's available). The Android, Windows and Linux apps are one app built from the same code: the same screens, settings and features. Pair a phone and a computer and the phone becomes its remote, and Sync keeps your plays, playlists and moods the same on both.

**Works best with:** a Tidal subscription plus your own song files. Tidal streams and downloads in FLAC; the other services bring your playlists and history over.

---

## Playing music

- **Now playing:** The big cover, with ☆ to favorite and ⋯ for options. Swipe the cover for the next or previous song, swipe up for the queue, tap it for lyrics, double-tap to pause.
- **Quality badge:** Says what you're hearing ("FLAC 24-bit/96 kHz", a stream's quality, or "Offline"). On a stream, **⤓ FLAC** saves it.
- **Queue:** What you added comes first, then the rest of what you started. Drag to reorder. **Undo** at its top takes back your last changes to it (cleared, shuffled, moved, removed or added), up to ten, without interrupting the song. Play next and Add to queue put a song in again if it is already waiting, or with **Move, don't duplicate** (Settings › Playback) move it there instead. With **Queues** on, each album, playlist and folder keeps its own queue where you left it; **Q** at the top of Now playing opens them.
- **Shuffle, Repeat and Autoplay:** Shuffle mixes the whole list, the songs already played too, with the one playing first. Settings › Playback › Shuffle picks how: **Random** is pure chance; **Smart** favors songs you love but haven't heard in a while; **Unheard and neglected** plays only songs you haven't heard in a long time or at all (the never played first, then the ones heard longest ago, loosely shuffled) and leaves a song you have heard out of every shuffle until the rest of that list has been heard too, across queues and restarts, so a shuffled list can be shorter than the list it came from. All three go by your plays everywhere, Last.fm and your imports too; Repeat one starts the song again reliably; Autoplay keeps similar songs coming when the queue ends. With the DJ on, only Repeat one is used: the DJ's sets go on in their own order.
- **Per song:** Its own volume (−12 to +12 dB), its own EQ and its own speed, remembered each time it plays.
- **Your copy first:** Wherever a song shows up (a playlist, search, a radio, the DJ), your own file plays when you have it, with its cover and tags.
- **Mono** puts both sides in one earbud; **casting** sends music to a Chromecast, Google speaker or TV.
- **It remembers:** the queue, where you were in the song, and every setting come back when the app starts again.

## Your library

Everything is in the ☰ menu. Each page can be a list, a grid or a compact grid, with its own search and sort, remembered.

- **My music, Albums, EPs, Singles, Artists, Genres and Folders,** from your files and downloads. An album lists its songs by track number (two discs: disc one, then disc two) and shows **Missing from this album** to download the rest.
- **Which tab a release is in:** a release tagged "… - Single" or "… - EP" is in Singles or EPs, in any folder; a folder named as an EP or a single decides too, and the Tagger can put a release in any of the three by hand.
- **Artist pages:** Their albums and singles first, then your songs (by album, or the sort you pick), **Every song** of theirs listed right under (all their releases, each song once, no button to press), a choice of which one when two bands share a name, **Featured on** (other artists' songs that feature them, as a list of songs), a menu for **Everything**, **Only my music** or **Only streaming**, picking several releases at once (hold one) to pin or hide them together, a **Sort** menu for their releases, your songs and Every song, releases only Genius lists added so the discography is whole, **Sort with Genius** when a service has mixed two bands under one artist (only what is on that artist's list on Genius stays), **Hide from this artist's page** in a song's Options or an album's menu to sort them yourself, **Show what's left out** to see and put back anything hidden, **Change picture…**, a **Discovery playlist** (their songs you don't have), an artist playlist, and **Merge into…** for an artist written two ways. The **Discovery** screen lists every artist's discovery playlists. **Listen later** (by Discovery) holds the songs, albums and EPs you put aside with **Add to Listen later playlist**. **Other device's songs** (Settings › Remote & sync) lists what your phone or PC has that this device hasn't, after a Sync, as a checklist to download or leave out.
- **Playlists:** Make, sort, fold into folders, import and export (.m3u8). A playlist in a folder shows in its folder only. **Smart playlists** fill themselves by rules (genre, mood, plays, format, skips, length…), with ready-made ones.
- **Unreleased:** your songs in any folder named Unreleased, and in the folders you put there with **Folders…**, with Play, Shuffle and the DJ for all of them.
- **More lists:** Favorites, Recently played, Downloads, 24-bit and 32-bit FLACs, Skips, Duplicates, and Pinned items at the top of the menu.
- **Refresh in every tab:** ↻ at the top reads your files again, or adds up your listening again, whichever the tab shows.
- **Select:** Hold down on a song (right-click on a computer) and picking starts with it picked; pick several songs, albums, playlists or folders, then play, queue, download, tag or delete them together. In Search, holding an artist or an album picks all its songs at once.
- **Search** ignores symbols ("acdc" finds AC/DC) and takes voice typing; Back returns to what you searched for.
- **Covers:** found for songs without one; a song with no picture in its file shows the cover in its folder (cover.jpg, folder.jpg…). **Covers from folders** (Settings › Scanner) puts that picture into each file that has none.
- **Moved files are followed,** so playlists still find them, and a song you have under another name of its artist isn't called missing.
- **Asked for at the start (phone):** your music, the microphone (for the visualizer; nothing is recorded), notifications and All files access, until each is allowed; and a music folder, until one is chosen.
- **Nothing is deleted without asking.**

## Streaming & downloads

- **Fetch the next song ahead:** while a streamed song plays, the one after it is fetched whole into the app's cache and played from there, so it starts with no wait and can't stall halfway. Nothing is kept. Settings › Playback switches it off.
- **Tidal:** Streams and downloads in FLAC; its favorites, playlists and radios work as a library.
- **Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer, YouTube Music:** Sign in to bring your library and playlists over (**Import all into my playlists**). Their songs play from Tidal, or YouTube when Tidal doesn't have one.
- **Tidal first, always:** with Tidal connected a song streams from Tidal in the quality you picked. If Tidal doesn't answer it is asked again before YouTube's audio is used, and a song that fell to YouTube is looked up on Tidal again the next time. Settings › Problems says when it happened.
- **SoundCloud:** Streaming only, with your own sign-in: search, your likes and playlists, artists and radio. Its songs stay in their own queue and can't be downloaded.
- **Search any of them** from the menu by the search bar, or paste a link from another app. **Genius** is there too, for music no service has: it finds artists, releases and singles by name (it has no music; they play if Tidal or YouTube has them).
- **Downloads:** Any song comes from Tidal with full tags, cover and genre, from the artist's own album first, into Music › Artists › the artist › the release. Whole playlists download too, skipping songs you have. A song you have already (the same title and artist, whatever its album) isn't downloaded again; a near match asks whether to keep both or replace.
- **Quality:** FLAC 24-bit, FLAC 16-bit or 320 kbps, set separately for streaming and downloads. **Clean or explicit** versions, wherever Tidal has both.
- **New releases:** Checks every artist you have for albums, EPs and singles from the last week, month or year.
- **The bell on New releases:** with New releases by themselves on (Settings › Playback), the app looks once a day while it is open and lists what is new under the bell on the New releases screen, with a count of how many came since you last opened it. No notification is sent. It looks at the artists of the songs you have as files or downloads and of the songs in your Radon Player playlists; a playlist that is only on a streaming service doesn't count.

## Moods

Every song gets the moods that fit it, from 12: Happy, Energetic, Party, Hype, Chill, Calm, Dreamy, Focus, Romantic, Sad, Dark and Intense (four a song at most). Its best fit is its main mood.

- **How the scan works:** **Scan moods** hears every song whole (its energy, tempo, brightness and key) and reads what its words mean, whole lines and sayings, not single words. The sound counts more for Energetic, Party and Chill, the words more for Sad, Romantic and Dark. Only once a song's sound and words are both done are its moods written into its file's MOOD tag.
- **Scanned once:** No scan starts by itself. Scan moods runs when you tap it, says about how long it will take, and shows the time left. Stop any time; it carries on from there.
- **AI reading (optional):** Add your own Anthropic API key and Claude reads the lyrics instead, catching irony and sad stories told cheerfully. **Test the key** says whether it works. The key stays on the device.
- **Fix one:** Tagger › Tags › Mood. Songs that sound like it learn from your fix. The Moods tab lists songs it's unsure of.
- **Starting over** (Settings › Reset mood and lyrics scans, each asks first): **Reset moods** works them out again from what was already heard and read; **Reset sound scan** has every song heard again; **Clear my moods** forgets the ones you picked; **Remove all moods** starts from scratch.
- **Prevent auto mood scans** (Settings › Scanner) freezes every mood as it is.
- **Used in:** Smart playlists, the DJ, Most played and Wrapped. Backups carry them, and **Moods match** (Settings › Remote & sync) puts one device's moods on the other.

## The DJ

Short sets built from *your* listening, with a voice like a radio host's, over the music.

- **How it works:** The DJ adds up everything you've played (Last.fm and your imports; the plays in the app when there are none) and makes one set after another, each of one kind, with a line to introduce it. It always plays from your whole library: your files, downloads, saved songs and every song in your playlists. With **Include new songs**, new songs like the ones you listen to come in too.
- **The kinds of set:** on repeat, this time of day, your most played, throwbacks, songs you loved and forgot, ten mood sets, a genre, new music like yours, deep cuts, an artist spotlight, an album moment (three in a row, in order), on this day years ago, a decade trip, one-hit wonders, not skipped this month, second chances, your unreleased songs and this week's streaks.
- **Your repeats:** four sets come from the songs you let play again with Repeat one: Can't let go (the most repeated), This month's obsessions, Old obsessions (about a year ago) and Like your repeats (songs close to them). With a song on Repeat one the DJ says so as it starts over: three and five in a row, its 10th, 25th, 50th… repeat, a new longest run, and after a long run an offer to carry on with something else. Before a song it may say how often you've had it on repeat.
- **Taking turns:** every kind gets its turn before any comes back, remembered across restarts. No song the DJ played comes back until it's played 300 others. Each set flows by mood, and no artist plays twice in a row. Skips never keep a song out; they're only counted.
- **The voice:** She says what's coming over the start of the song, the music turned down under her, never paused (bit-perfect to a USB DAC too): a hello for the time and day, milestones (a song's 100th play), and now and then a word before a song. The natural voice is the same on the phone and the computer; it's fetched once (about 95 MB) from DJ › Voice settings.
- **Facts about your listening:** between sets, now and then: time spent with a song, when you first played it, your top artists and moods, the hours you listen at, your biggest year, your collection's size, what you skip. The subjects take turns, and nothing is said twice until the rest has been.
- **Instant:** The first set is ready before you tap DJ, and more are kept ready with their lines. **Switch it up** (✦ on Now playing) jumps to another kind; from your headphones, pause then Next does the same.
- **Ask for something:** Type or say an artist, a genre, a decade, a mood or a vibe ("workout").

## Stats & Wrapped

- **How plays are counted:** from Last.fm and the histories you import (Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer, YouTube Music, Tidal), added together and never twice. A song is the same song across all of them, featurings and punctuation aside (K.Flay = KFlay). Imports are a one-time picture; Last.fm brings the new plays. Without Last.fm, the plays in the app count.
- **Most played:** Top songs, albums, artists and moods for a week, month, year or all time, by plays or by time, with charts of when you listen. Plus Repeat, Milestones and Skips.
- **Repeats:** each time a song plays to its end and starts over by itself with Repeat one on is one repeat of it. The Repeats tab lists your songs by repeats, with each one's longest run, for all time, a year, 30 days or 7, and makes a collage of them. Repeats from before the app counted them, and from other services, are worked out from what you played here, your Last.fm scrobbles and your imports (a song played again the moment it ended): found by themselves after an import and kept; a good guess, not a record. Apple Music's daily counts and library exports have no exact times, so only its Play Activity file can show them. The chart of what you played most lately is **On repeat**.
- **Unplayed and neglected:** a tab (under Statistics) of the songs of yours you have never played, and of the ones not played in 3 months, 6 months, a year or 2 years, the longest ago first, each with when it was last played and its plays. Play or Shuffle them from there. It counts your stats (Last.fm and your imports) and the plays here; a song with plays but no date is in neither list.
- **Tidy stats:** Merge an artist or a song counted under two names, say whose plays a joint credit's are, and keep look-alike artists apart.
- **Wrapped:** Any period as full-screen slides: top 15s (songs, artists, albums, singles, EPs, genres, moods), minutes, streaks, discoveries, your listener type and a summary card. Every slide saves or shares as a picture.
- **Repeats in Wrapped:** how many and how long, your biggest obsession, your most repeated songs and artists (full lists), your longest run, your repeat day, when you repeat most, each month's most repeated song, and the kind of repeater you are.
- **Wrapped styles:** Pick how the slides look, including **Your look** (the app's look in use) and the ones you made.

## Lyrics

- **Synced** lyrics light up line by line (tap a line to jump there); **Genius** shows them as written, with annotations.
- **Lyrics card:** Tick up to 25 lines for a picture to share or save, in Your look, Now playing's colours, 60 ready-made styles, or your own. Tick the first line (it is marked 1) and the last (marked 2) and every line between is ticked with them; a ticked line unticks on its own, a third line starts another run, and Clear starts over.
- **With the song's sound:** tick it in the Lyrics card box and the card is shared as a short video with the song playing, from the first line ticked to where the last one stops; Starts and Ends set it yourself, and are how it is set for a song nobody has timed lyrics for.
- **Share a clip with sound:** a song's Options makes a short video of its cover, name and a seek bar that fills, with the song playing from where you say to where you say (3 seconds to a minute), in any lyrics card style. For a song you only stream the sound is taken from its stream. Once a clip is made it is kept while its box is open, so it can go to one app, then another, then be saved without being made again; changing the clip makes a new one, and closing the box throws the kept one away (a saved copy stays).
- **Plays on cards:** lyrics cards, picture cards and clips say how often you've played the song beside the artist (Personalization switches it off).
- **Lyrics scanner:** Finds lyrics for every song and writes them into the files; the ones it can't find you can type or mark instrumental.

## Sound

- **Equalizer:** One for each output (speaker, wired headphones, each Bluetooth device, a USB DAC, the car), switched in by itself. 3 to 31 bands, presets, your own presets, and **Auto EQ** headphone correction by name. Each output keeps everything as you left it.
- **Boost, Crossfeed and Crossfade,** with smart transitions that never fade inside an album that flows. **Dual volume** decides whether a song's own volume and the Boost go together.
- **Sound quality:** clip guard, smoothing of harsh loud masters, dither, high-quality resampling and detail (on from the start, each can be turned off), and loudness compensation (off from the start).
- **Bit-perfect** to a USB DAC (phone) or the computer's sound card: each song reaches the DAC untouched, at its own sample rate and bit depth (a 24-bit song always as 24-bit). On phones whose Android has no bit-perfect mode (Samsung's), Radon Player takes the DAC over and streams to it itself. **Bass and Treble with bit-perfect** keeps just those two working, with headroom so nothing clips.
- **The DAC's own EQ** (a FiiO KA17; phone and Windows): turn the PEQ stored in the DAC off and on, or pick its preset, without FiiO Control.
- **Bluetooth:** **Ignore Bluetooth devices** so the car doesn't take the music; **Newest Bluetooth device wins**; **Car always wins** (phone).

## Personalization

Settings › Personalization (or the palette on Now playing) changes how the app looks, Wrapped's style and the lyrics card's style included. Each menu shows every choice as live screens to swipe through (Now playing with its visualizer moving, My music, Albums, slides of Wrapped, the lyrics card), and each device keeps its own choices.

- **Looks** are the colours: Light, Dark, **Now playing** (every screen in the playing cover's colours), and over ninety themes: light, dark, neon, and thirty looks after materials and places (Stained glass, Blueprint, Tea house, Kite sky…), each with an effect of its own.
- **Themes** are how things are drawn: 45 of them (Rounded, Sharp, Compact, Glass, Retro, Brutalist, Storybook, Ledger…), with any look.
- **Layouts** are where things are: the ☰ menu, a bottom bar, a side rail or top tabs, the mini player's style, and 6 ways to arrange Now playing: 45 ready-made layouts.
- **Effects:** 91 to pick from (twinkling stars, aurora, rain, snow, synthwave grid, nebula, petals, lightning, a moon, warp speed, stained glass, paper cranes, lanterns, kites…), each in its own colour.
- **Presets:** 60 ready-made setups that go together (Midnight drive, Northern lights, Cherry blossom, Code rain, Vaporwave, Cathedral, Drawing office, Tea ceremony, Windy day…), each with its own look, theme, layout, effects, visualizer, Wrapped style and lyrics card style. **Save as a preset** keeps yours. **Preset Shuffle** puts on a different favourite every 5 to 30 minutes.
- **Follows the cover:** a preset that changes with every song: the cover's own colours blurred out behind see-through cards, and a scene to match (effects and two visualizers in the cover's colour) out of 17, one for any colour a cover can be.
- **Glass cover:** a preset with the playing song's cover blurred behind the whole app and everything over it see-through.
- **Presets and your visualizers:** two switches under Preset Shuffle make presets leave Now playing's middle visualizer, its bottom one, or both as you set them, so a preset can bring only its looks.
- **Select several, Hide:** every Personalization menu starts with Select several: tick as many as you like, then Hide them (they go under Hidden at the menu's end, a tap brings one back), Copy codes, or, for presets, Edit. The one in use is never hidden.
- **Edit the ready-made presets:** take effects off or add them, and set the middle and bottom visualizers each to as it came, None or another style; the look and the rest stay. Reset default preset edits, at the end of every menu, puts them all back. Nothing ready-made is ever deleted.
- **Share codes:** Copy code on any choice of any menu gives one line of text for someone else (or your other device) to paste under Share. A preset goes whole, with what it uses; also everything on now, and your effects.
- **Menus in sections:** each Personalization menu is folded into a few sections (Looks: Basics, Light, Dark, Neon, Materials and places; Effects: Sky, Nature, Patterns, Light, Things…), your favourites first, so a long list is a short one to start with.
- **Favorite, Edit, Delete:** Hold down on any choice (or its ⋯, or right-click). ★ Favorite moves it to the top.
- **Make your own:** a **look** (colours, fade, glow, stars and effects), a **layout** (the bar's buttons, the mini player, Now playing's arrangement), a **visualizer** (shape, mirror, spin, glow, colour blend, **Dual**), a **lyrics card** and a **Wrapped** style, each shown live as you change it. **Randomize** and **Saved colours** give you a start.
- **Yours everywhere:** everything you make and favourite goes into backups and to your other device at each Sync.

## Visualizer

- **90 styles** and over eighty colours and blends: ones along the bottom (bars, waves, city lights, flames, meadow, 8-bit…) and ones around a middle (rings, starfield, galaxy, tunnel, orbits, supernova, mandala, heart…), and thirty that are things: organ pipes, an abacus, piano keys, a rose window, a lotus, a snow crystal, a kite.
- **On Now playing:** Slim bars, any style or yours, or **Dual**: one style in the middle and another along the bottom at once, or **full screen** behind everything. It only hears the app's own sound.
- **Smoothest screen (phone, Settings › Playback):** asks a 90, 120 or 144 Hz screen for its highest rate while the app is on screen. Off by default.

## Tags & tidying

- **Tagger:** A song's tags (with **Find tags online**, which shows every change first), cover, info, lyrics, mood, and which tab a release goes in. Changed artist or album? Its file moves to match. On the phone it works on any of your files once you allow All files access.
- **Scanner tools** (Settings › Scanner): Fix genre names, Duplicates, Upgrade to FLAC, Songs without covers, Identify by sound and Similar artists.
- **Computer only:** Fix albums and artists, Empty folders, and folders to leave alone.

## Phone + computer together

- **Phone remote:** Turn on Phone remote on the computer, enter its code on the phone, and the phone plays, queues, skips and sets the volume there.
- **Sync now:** Tap it on either device: plays, skips, favorites, EQs, downloads, tag changes and synced playlists (⇄) come together, nothing counted twice, with **Undo last sync**.
- **Playlists that sync:** A list of all your playlists with a switch each, smart playlists, Listen later and the discovery playlists among them. New playlists sync by themselves (Settings › Your files › New playlists sync turns that off), and every list of playlists marks each one ⇄ SYNCED (with CHANGES WAITING when you changed it since the last sync) or THIS DEVICE ONLY. The ones switched on are the same on both devices after each Sync (two of the same name become one with the songs of both, as do Listen later and an artist's discovery playlists); the rest stay where they are.
- **Send a playlist:** Copies one playlist (or Listen later, or a discovery playlist) to the other device once, without syncing it, and names any songs the other device hasn't got.
- **Auto playlist sync:** A switch: the synced playlists sync by themselves when the phone's app finds the PC's, and when one changes while both are open. Everything else waits for Sync now.
- **Tags match:** Pick a main device and the songs on the other get the tags theirs are missing (title, artist, album, genre, year, track), except folders you tell sync to leave alone. It never touches moods.
- **Moods match:** The same for moods only, with its own main device: at each sync the other device's songs get the main one's moods, written into their files, with nothing scanned again. So the tags can follow the phone and the moods the PC. Off, each device keeps its own moods.
- **Continue where you left off** on the other device, and **back up and restore** everything in one file.

## Phone only

- **Android Auto** with covers, Favorite and Shuffle, and the car's own EQ.
- **Lock screen player, notification and home screen widget,** in the song's colours, a picture-in-picture mini player, and **Auto mono** for chosen earbuds.
- **Download from the notification:** in Settings, switch off Shuffle button in the notification. Then, while a streamed song you don't have is playing, a Download button takes Shuffle's place in the notification and on the lock screen.

## Computer only (Windows and Linux)

- **System tray** and an always-on-top **mini player**: Now playing made small, with your look, effects, visualizer and queue.
- **Media keys:** the keyboard's and a headset's play, next and previous keys, and the system's own "now playing" controls (Windows' media flyout; on Linux the desktop's controls, through MPRIS).
- **Start with Windows / Start with Linux** (Settings › General): in the tray when you sign in.
- **Discord** shows what you're playing on your profile.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** (Space, ←/→, W for Wrapped, E for the Equalizer, / to search) and right-click menus everywhere.
- **Linux:** new in 4.0, as a .deb package that brings mpv and ffmpeg with it. Where a desktop has no tray, closing the window minimizes it. **Software drawing** (Settings › General) is for a PC or virtual machine where the window flickers. Not on Linux yet: the DAC's own EQ and dictation.

## Help

- **Favorite settings:** the first section of Settings holds the settings you starred (the ☆ beside a setting's name), for the ones you use all the time. Each stays in its own section too.
- **Size of everything (Settings › General):** makes the app's text and everything else bigger or smaller together, from 80% to 250%, for a screen where it all comes out too small or too big. On the PC it changes at once; on the phone the screen is drawn again.
- **Updates:** Checked each time the app starts (it says whether there's one); **Update** in Settings installs it (on Linux it downloads the package and opens it with your system's installer).
- **Changelog and Features** are in the ☰ menu.
- **Problems:** Settings shows anything that went wrong recently, with a Copy button.
