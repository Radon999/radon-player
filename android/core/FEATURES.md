# Radon Player

**All your music in one place, on your phone and your computer.**

Radon Player plays your own music files and the streaming services you use, together, in FLAC (hi-res where it's available). The Android, Windows and Linux apps are one app: the same screens, settings and features. Pair a phone and a computer and the phone becomes its remote, and Sync keeps your plays, playlists and choices the same on both.

**Works best with:** a Tidal subscription plus your own song files. Tidal streams and downloads in FLAC; the other services bring your playlists and history over.

Almost everything here is a choice: where something can be switched off or changed, it says so.

---

## Playing music

- **Now playing:** the big cover, with ☆ to favorite and ⋯ for options. Swipe the cover for the next or previous song, swipe up for the queue, tap for lyrics, double-tap to pause.
- **Quality badge:** says exactly what you're hearing ("FLAC 24-bit/96 kHz", a stream's quality, "Offline"). On a stream, **⤓ FLAC** saves it.
- **Your copy first:** wherever a song turns up (a playlist, search, a radio, the DJ), your own file plays if you have it, with its cover and tags, even when your history names it a little differently. For an unreleased song listed under another name, **This is a song of mine…** (a song's Options) links it to your file once.
- **Streaming or on this device:** every song says where it plays from: its format ("FLAC 24", "MP3", ⤓ for a download) or **Streaming**.
- **Shuffle, three ways** (Settings › Playback › Shuffle): **Random** is pure chance; **Smart** favours songs you love but haven't heard in a while; **Unheard and neglected** plays only what you've never played or not for a long time. Whichever you pick, a song you've heard waits until the rest of the list has had its turn, across queues and restarts; a skipped song doesn't count as heard.
- **Repeat and Autoplay:** repeat a song or the list. Autoplay keeps similar songs coming when the queue ends; off, the music stops there. With the DJ on, only Repeat one is used.
- **Autoplay that knows you:** the songs autoplay and song radios bring are put in an order that fits you: songs you often play after the one playing, artists you love and the ones you play alongside them, your own copies, songs that sound alike (tempo and loudness) or share the mood or genre, and the artists you play at this time of day and on this output (the car, earbuds, the PC). Left out: anything you heard in the last three hours, songs you keep skipping (above all in their first seconds), and what you said no to. No artist twice in a row.
- **More like this, Less like this, Not for autoplay:** in a song's Options and an artist's menu. Autoplay, radios and the DJ follow them, and they sync between your phone and computer.
- **Per song:** its own volume (−12 to +12 dB), EQ and speed, remembered.
- **Mono** puts both sides in one earbud; **casting** sends music to a Chromecast, a Google speaker or a TV.
- **It remembers** the queue, your place in the song and every setting.

### The queue

- **Yours first:** what you add comes first, then the rest of what you started. Drag to reorder.
- **More than one queue:** switch **Queues** on (Settings › Your music) and every album, playlist and folder keeps its own queue where you left it; **Q** on Now playing lists them to jump between. Off, there's one queue.
- **Undo** at its top takes back a clear, a shuffle, a move, a removal or an addition, up to ten steps, without stopping the song.
- **Add twice, or not:** Play next and Add to queue put a song in again if it's already waiting; **Move, don't duplicate** (Settings › Playback) moves it instead.

## Your library

Everything is in the ☰ menu, and **Menu entries** (Settings) takes out what you never use. Each page is a list, a grid or a compact grid, with its own search and sort, remembered.

- **My music, Albums, EPs, Singles, Artists, Genres, Folders,** from your files and downloads. Albums has a second tab, **Low song count albums** (one to four songs here). A release tagged "- Single" or "- EP", or in a folder named so, goes in that tab; the Tagger can move one by hand.
- **Plays everywhere:** every song, album, artist, playlist, genre, folder and mood shows how often you've played it.
- **What you have of an album:** each album says how many of its songs are on this device and how many you'd only stream. **Missing from this album** lists the rest to play, put on Listen later, or download.
- **Artist pages:** their albums and singles, your songs, **Every song** of theirs (each once), and **Featured on**. Show everything, only your music or only streaming; sort, pin or hide releases (**Show what's left out** puts them back); **Sort with Genius** when a service mixed two bands under one name; change the picture; **Merge into…** for an artist written two ways. Plus their Discovery playlist (their songs you don't have) and an artist playlist.
- **Playlists:** make, sort, fold into folders, give a cover, import and export (.m3u8). **Your own files first:** a streaming song you have as your own file or download goes in as your file, and one already in a playlist is swapped for it. **Rearrange songs** (a playlist's Options) puts its songs in your own order, and **Rearrange…** (Your order) the playlists themselves: drag them, or move one place at a time. **Find duplicates** (every playlist's buttons) lists the songs in it more than once, the same way Duplicates finds them, the copy that plays from this device first, each copy with **Keep** or **Remove**, and **Not a duplicate**; Remove only takes a copy out of the playlist, your files stay. **Smart playlists** fill themselves by rules (genre, mood, plays, format, skips, length…), with ready-made ones.
- **Listen later:** put a song, album or EP aside. **Review** lists the ones you haven't got, to download, keep streaming or remove.
- **More lists:** Favorites, Recently played, Downloads, Skips, Duplicates, **Unreleased** (your songs in folders named Unreleased, or ones you add), and Pinned at the top of the menu.
- **File type:** a tab for each kind of file you have (FLAC, 24-bit and 32-bit FLAC, MP3, M4A, WAV, Ogg…), for when one kind needs fixing or tagging.
- **Pick several:** hold a song (right-click on a computer), then pick songs, albums, playlists or folders to play, queue, download, tag or delete together. In Search, holding an artist or album picks all its songs.
- **Search** ignores symbols ("acdc" finds AC/DC) and takes voice; Back returns to your search. **Recent searches:** the Search tab and every page's own search (Albums, Artists, a playlist…) each keep their own last ten, shown under the empty search bar: tap one to search it again, × to forget it. **↻** in any tab reads your files again.
- **Covers looked after:** found for songs without one (Deezer, your service, Last.fm, iTunes, Genius), or taken from the folder's picture.
- **Moved files are followed,** so playlists still find them, even a file named with its track number ("11 Ruby") or with no artist. **A song that can't be played is taken away:** a file gone from this device is linked to your copy of it, else it streams, and one that's on neither is taken out of your playlists, the queue and favorites (listed in Settings › Problems). **Nothing is deleted without asking.**

## Streaming & downloads

- **Tidal:** streams and downloads in FLAC; its favorites, playlists and radios work as a library. **Tidal first:** a song only falls back to YouTube's audio when Tidal doesn't answer, and Tidal is tried again next time.
- **Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer, YouTube Music:** sign in to bring your library and playlists over (**Import all into my playlists**); their songs play from Tidal, or YouTube when Tidal doesn't have one.
- **SoundCloud** with your own sign-in: search, likes, playlists, artists and radio. Streaming only.
- **Search any of them,** or paste a link from another app. **Genius** finds artists and releases no service lists (they play if Tidal or YouTube has them).
- **Downloads:** from Tidal with full tags, cover and genre, into Music › Artists › the artist › the release. Whole playlists too, skipping what you have. A song you already have isn't downloaded twice; for a near match, **Copies** (Settings › Your music) decides: ask, keep both or replace.
- **Upgrade** (Settings › Your music): swaps your MP3s and other lossy files for Tidal's FLAC, and 16-bit ones when Tidal has them in 24-bit. Playlists keep working, and the old files go to the Recycle Bin.
- **Quality:** FLAC 24-bit, FLAC 16-bit or 320 kbps, set separately for streaming and downloads, and **Clean or explicit** where Tidal has both. Not signed in to Tidal, the app says so when it starts (streams are YouTube's audio, about 160 kbps Opus, until you sign in), with **Sign in to Tidal**, **Not now** and **Don't show again** for using it with your own files only.
- **No waiting between songs:** the next streamed song is fetched while this one plays (**Fetch the next song ahead**, Settings › Playback; switch it off to save data).
- **New releases** from the artists you have, for the last week, month or year (streaming services only). **New releases by themselves** (Settings › Playback) checks once a day while the app is open and shows a count on the bell; no notification.
- **Other device's songs** (Settings › Remote & sync): after a Sync, the songs your other device has and this one hasn't, as a checklist to download or leave out.

## Moods

Every song gets up to four moods from twelve: Happy, Energetic, Party, Hype, Chill, Calm, Dreamy, Focus, Romantic, Sad, Dark and Intense. The best fit is its main mood.

- **How:** **Scan moods** (the Moods tab) hears each song whole (energy, tempo, brightness, key) and reads what its words mean, then writes the moods into the file's MOOD tag.
- **Only when you ask:** a scan runs when you tap it, shows the time left, and can be stopped and carried on. A song is scanned once, and one whose file already has moods (from your other device, say) is left as it is.
- **AI reading (optional):** add your own Anthropic API key and Claude reads the lyrics, catching irony and sad stories told cheerfully. Without one, it works on its own.
- **Fix one** in the Tagger, and songs that sound like it learn from your fix.
- **Queued mood edits** (Utilities): select songs anywhere (in a DJ set, say) and pick **Queue a mood edit**; look at them all later, tick and untick their moods, and **Save to the file**, written into the files as the Tagger does.
- **The sound check:** worked-out moods that the sound plainly says otherwise of (a loud, fast song only Chill, a soft, slow one only Hype) follow the sound. **Check moods against the sound** (Queued mood edits) hears your songs, only their sound (no words, no AI), and lists the ones whose moods in the file don't fit it, each with what it should be and why; **Use it**, or **Use every suggestion**.
- **Your controls:** **Prevent auto mood scans** freezes every mood; Settings › Reset mood and lyrics scans starts over (moods only, the sound scan, your own picks, or everything).
- **Used in** smart playlists, the DJ, Most played and Wrapped, and they go across with Sync and backups.

## The DJ

Short sets from your own listening, with a voice like a radio host's.

- **Sets of one kind each:** on repeat, this time of day, most played, throwbacks, loved and forgotten, ten mood sets, a genre, new music like yours, deep cuts, an artist spotlight, an album moment, on this day, a decade trip, one-hit wonders, second chances, your unreleased songs, streaks, and four from the songs you repeat.
- **From your whole library,** files, downloads and playlists. **Include new songs** brings in new music like yours; off, only what you have.
- **New music picked from your taste:** new songs start from the songs you love most (heard to the end, favourites, More like this, repeats, artists you've just fallen for) as well as recent ones; artists suggested by several of your favourites come first, and ones you've drifted from don't lead anywhere. What you said no to, songs you skip early and artists whose new songs you keep skipping are left out, and the rest is ranked against your taste before the set is picked.
- **It takes turns:** every kind plays before one comes back, no song returns until 300 others have played, no artist twice in a row, and each set flows by mood and in the order you tend to play things (what you usually play after what).
- **The voice** talks over the start of the song with the music turned down, never paused: a hello, milestones (a song's 100th play), a word about a song or your repeats, facts about your listening. Each thing has a few wordings, taking turns. The natural voice (about 95 MB, from DJ › Voice settings) is the same on phone and computer; or use the phone's own voice, or none.
- **Instant:** the first set is ready before you tap DJ. **Switch it up** (✦) jumps to another kind; on headphones, pause then Next does the same.
- **Ask for something:** an artist, a genre, a decade, a mood or "workout".

## Stats & Wrapped

- **Your plays, from everywhere:** Last.fm and the histories you import (Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer, YouTube Music, Tidal), added together and never twice; a song is one song across all of them. Without Last.fm, the app's own plays count.
- **Bring your history:** Settings › Last.fm & your stats takes each service's data file (it says where to get it). Importing again adds only new plays; **Reset plays** undoes a wrong import. Spotify's history also brings your skips.
- **Most played:** top songs, albums, artists and moods for any period, by plays or time, with charts of when you listen. Plus Milestones, Skips, On this day and **On repeat**.
- **Repeats:** each time a song starts over on Repeat one counts, with its longest run, for all time, a year, 30 or 7 days, and a collage. Older repeats are worked out from your history (Apple Music only from its Play Activity file, the one with exact times).
- **Unplayed and neglected:** songs you've never played, or not in 3 months to 2 years, to play or shuffle.
- **Milestones** (Statistics): every milestone you've reached, each song and artist getting to 100, 250, 500, 1,000… plays, with the day it happened. Songs, artists or both; newest first (by year) or biggest first. Tap a song of yours to play it, an artist to open their page.
- **Taste** (Statistics): what autoplay, radios and the DJ learn from. Your phases (new loves, rising, long-time favourites, faded), the artists you skip most and songs skipped in their first seconds, the songs you always hear to the end, the new songs the DJ and autoplay brought and how they went (kept, heard out or skipped; artists whose new songs keep getting skipped aren't brought again), and everything you said, each undone with a tap.
- **Skips:** pressing Next with more than 15 seconds left. Going back, picking another song or the app moving on aren't skips.
- **Tidy stats** (Settings › Last.fm & your stats): **Similar artists** (merge an artist written two ways, or keep them apart), **Duplicates in Most played** (one song counted under two names), **Joint artists** (whose plays a duet's are). Each has **Never show** for one that's right as it is, with Undo and Show them again.
- **Hide from stats:** long-press (right-click) a song, album or artist in a stats list, or a row in Wrapped; it's no longer shown, in the stats lists or anywhere in Wrapped (top lists, Never skipped and Most skipped, Repeats, milestones, each month's top), and the next one moves up into its place straight away. Settings › Hidden from stats brings it back.
- **Wrapped,** for a week, month, year or all time: top 15 songs, artists, albums, singles, EPs, genres and moods, minutes, streaks, discoveries, obsessions and repeats, your listener type and a summary card. Every slide saves or shares as a picture, in a style you pick or make. Long-press a wrong cover to pick another.
- **Collages** of your top covers, and **Export / Import** of the app's own play history (Settings › Accounts).

## Lyrics

- **Synced lyrics** light up line by line (tap a line to jump); **Genius** shows them as written, with annotations.
- **Edit lyrics** in the Lyrics tab (the words, and the times of synced lines): saved straight into the song's file. The song playing gets them the moment it ends (shown at once); a file the app may not change, or a streamed song, keeps them in the app.
- **Lyrics cards:** tick up to 25 lines (tick the first and last to get every line between) for a picture to share or save, in one of 60 styles, your look, Now playing's colours, or your own.
- **With the song's sound:** the card as a short video with the song playing over the lines you ticked, or between Starts and Ends you set yourself. Untick it for a plain picture.
- **Clips with sound:** a song's Options makes a short video of its cover and a filling seek bar, with the song playing from where you say to where you say (3 seconds to a minute). Streamed songs too.
- **Picture cards** share a song with a line of your own. Cards and clips show your play count (Personalization switches it off).
- **Lyrics scanner:** finds lyrics for every song on this device and writes them into the files; type the ones it can't find, or mark them instrumental. Streamed songs aren't in it (there's no file to write to): their lyrics are found as they play and kept in the app, offline too. **Lyrics but no timing** is at the top of its screen. **Lyrics files (.lrc)** goes through every song each time and replaces the .lrc files with the lyrics in the songs' files, so edits reach them.
- **Auto-sync:** after the scan, the songs with words but no timing are listed apart from the ones with no lyrics. **Start auto-syncing missing synced lyrics** looks each one up once more, and the ones nobody has timed (your unreleased songs) it listens to on the device, hears when each line is sung, and times them itself. **Listen for their lyrics** does the songs with no lyrics at all: it writes down the words it hears, timed (English songs; a first go to correct with Edit). The lyrics go into the song's file, so Sync takes them to your other device. Songs done stay done and are listed; each start only does the ones left. The Lyrics tab marks them **Timed by Radon** or **Heard by Radon**, with **Time again** / **Listen again** and **Remove**. Phone and PC, each its own songs; the listening model is a one-time download of about 100 MB.

## Sound

- **Equalizer for each output:** speaker, wired headphones, each Bluetooth device, a USB DAC, the car, switched by itself. 3 to 31 bands, presets and your own, and **Auto EQ** headphone correction by name.
- **Presets for each output:** under the output picked in the Equalizer (Now playing's and the tab's), a drop-down with its name lists its own presets. **Save as preset…** keeps its whole EQ (Sound, bands, correction, bass and treble, Boost) under a name, and can make it the **default** (★): switched in each time the sound goes to that output. The presets and defaults of the outputs both devices meet (Bluetooth headphones and speakers, a USB DAC, the car) sync; all of them go into backups.
- **Boost, Crossfeed and Crossfade** (off until you set it; never inside an album that flows). **Dual volume** decides whether a song's own volume and Boost go together.
- **Bit-perfect** to a USB DAC (phone) or the computer's sound card: each song arrives untouched at its own rate and bit depth. On phones without a bit-perfect mode (Samsung's) the app drives the DAC itself. **Bass and Treble** can stay on with it. Off, the EQ and the rest come back.
- **Sound quality** (each its own switch): clip guard, smoothing of harsh loud masters, dither, high-quality resampling and detail (on at first), and loudness compensation (off at first). On the phone, **Android's own FLAC decoder** for phones whose maker's decoder drops sound now and then.
- **A FiiO KA17's own EQ** switched from the app (phone and Windows).
- **Bluetooth rules,** all optional: **Ignore Bluetooth devices** (so the car doesn't take the music), **Newest Bluetooth device wins**, **Car always wins** (phone). An ignored device coming or going can cause a moment's stutter, but the music always carries on by itself, even when the car or the earbuds send a pause of their own just then. For no stutter at all, Settings shows how to switch the car's Media audio off in Android's Bluetooth settings (Calls stay on). Switching Ignore Bluetooth devices off gives the car the music, and the phone pauses and plays once a few seconds after the car is connected, so the car's sound starts (if it doesn't, pause and play yourself).

## Personalization

Settings › Personalization (or the palette on Now playing). Every menu shows each choice as live screens to swipe through (an effect with the others you have on, as they would look together), and each device keeps its own.

- **Looks** (the colours): Light, Dark, **Now playing** (every screen in the cover's colours) and over ninety more, neon, and places and materials (Stained glass, Blueprint, Tea house…), and **Music scenes**: thirty looks of kinds of music (Jazz club, Punk, Disco, Techno, Concert hall…), each with an effect that moves to the music.
- **Themes** (how things are drawn): 45, from Rounded and Glass to Retro and Brutalist, with any look.
- **Layouts** (where things are): the ☰ menu, a bottom bar, a side rail or top tabs, the mini player, and how Now playing is arranged: 45 ready-made.
- **Effects:** 121, from stars, aurora and rain to lanterns and kites, each in its own colour. Thirty **move to the music** (beat rings, lasers, a disco ball, fireworks, a heart monitor…), as the visualizers do.
- **Presets:** 90 ready-made setups of everything together (Midnight drive, Cherry blossom, Cathedral…), thirty of them for the Music scenes (Smoky jazz, Mirror ball, Warehouse, The drop…), each with effects that move to the music and a visualizer round the cover. **Follows the cover** changes with every song; **Glass cover** blurs the cover behind the whole app. **Preset Shuffle** puts on another favourite every 5 to 30 minutes, and **Presets by time of day** picks one for each part of the day; both off until you turn them on. Two switches make presets leave your middle or bottom visualizer alone.
- **Your menus, your way:** **Select several** to hide choices (under Hidden, a tap brings them back) or copy their codes; ★ favourites go to the top. Edit a ready-made preset's effects and visualizers; **Reset default preset edits** puts them back. Nothing ready-made is ever deleted.
- **Colours** (Personalization › Colours): every colour in one place. Every visualizer at once, or each one in use on its own (Now playing's, its bottom one, the Visualizer tab's), and each effect you have on. A visualizer you made keeps the colour you made it with as its default, and **As it comes** puts that back; no need to make or edit one to change its colour. Scenes from the cover overrides them while it's on.
- **Make your own:** a look, a layout, a visualizer (shape, mirror, spin, glow, colours, or **Dual**), a lyrics card, a clip and a Wrapped style, each shown live, with **Randomize** to start from. Save the whole setup as a preset, or with **Save edits to…** over one of yours (the one put on last first).
- **Share codes:** **Copy code** on any choice gives one line of text to paste under Share on someone else's app (or your other device). What you make goes into backups and to your other device at each Sync.

## Visualizer

- **90 styles** and over eighty colours and blends: along the bottom (bars, blocks, waves, flames, 8-bit…), round the cover (rings, starfield, galaxy, mandala…), and thirty that are things (organ pipes, piano keys, a lotus, a kite).
- **On Now playing:** slim bars, any style, **Dual** (one round the cover and one along the bottom), full screen behind everything, or none. With **Dual** on, **In the middle** and **Along the bottom** each have their own picker. **Around the cover** (a switch, then a picker of 30: glow, bars, waves, ripples, flames, orbits, sparkles and more) goes round the cover from its edge outward, never over it, in the cover's shape and size from the layout (square, rounded, a circle like a record); while it's on, no middle visualizer is drawn, the bottom one still is. Make your own from them too (Make your own, with **Around the cover** on: only the ones made to go round the cover are offered); yours go in its menu, and Dual's **In the middle** offers them all. It only hears the app's own sound; nothing is recorded.
- **Smoothest screen** (phone, Settings › Playback): asks a 90, 120 or 144 Hz screen for its highest rate. Off at first.

## Tags & tidying

- **Tagger:** a song's tags (**Find tags online** shows every change first), cover, info, lyrics, mood, and which tab a release goes in. Change the artist or album and the file moves to match. On the phone, any of your files once you allow All files access.
- **Scanner tools** (Settings › Scanner): **Fix albums and artists**, **Live in song names**, **Soundtrack notes in names**, **Fix genre names**, **Duplicates** (a song in more than one file; a track number or signs in front of a name, "11 Ruby", "11. Ruby", "(11) Ruby", and a file with no artist don't hide one, though those are never marked Remove until you say so), **Songs without covers**, **Covers from folders** and **Save covers into folders**, **Identify by sound** and **Empty folders**. Each shows what it would change first, and has **Never show** for what's right as it is. **Folders to leave alone** keeps chosen folders out of every scan. On the phone they work on your own files with All files access.
- **Storage and damaged files** (Settings › Scanner): how much room your music takes, and any damaged song files. **Full check** reads every file through and finds every damaged spot, with **Listen** to hear it. **Show replacements** shows what each would be replaced with; **Download new copies** gets exactly those, keeping your playlists and plays.
- **Safe writes:** a tag or cover change is made on a copy, checked, then put in place, and never to the song playing or the next one. **Prevent album cover changes** (Settings › Scanner) stops every cover change.

## Phone + computer together

- **Sync now** (Settings › Remote & sync), on either device: plays, skips, favorites, EQs, downloads, tag edits, covers you picked, synced playlists, and every choice you made in the scans and stats screens (Never show included) come together, nothing counted twice, with **Undo last sync**. The main device's choices win.
- **Playlists that sync:** a switch for each playlist (smart playlists and Listen later too), marked ⇄ SYNCED, CHANGES WAITING or THIS DEVICE ONLY. New playlists sync by themselves unless you switch that off. **Auto playlist sync** does it whenever both apps are open.
- **Send a playlist** to the other device once, without syncing it.
- **Tags match** and **Moods match:** pick a main device, and the other device's copies of your songs are made the same (title, artist, album, genre, year, lyrics, moods, cover). Only what's certain is changed; nothing is guessed.
- **Phone remote:** play, queue, skip and set the volume on the computer from the phone (switch it on on the computer and enter its code).
- **Continue where you left off** on the other device, and **back up and restore** everything in one file (**Back up by itself** daily or weekly, if you like).

## Phone only

- **Android Auto** with covers, Favorite and Shuffle, and the car's own EQ.
- **Lock screen player, notification and home screen widget** in the song's colours, and a picture-in-picture mini player. With **Shuffle button in the notification** off, a Download button takes its place on a streamed song.
- **Swipe a song** to queue, favorite or more; you choose what each side does (or nothing).
- **Auto mono** for the earbuds you choose: the app's own mono, so Android's Mono audio setting (Accessibility) isn't changed and still shows off, but the music plays in mono.
- **Keep playing** (Settings › Playback, on at first): no other app pauses the music, not even one that takes the sound and plays nothing (a car connecting nearby). One that takes it for a moment (voice typing, the assistant, a spoken direction) turns the music down instead, and a call pauses it until the call is over. Off: Android's way.

## Computer only (Windows and Linux)

- **Mini player** that stays on top, with your look, effects, visualizer and queue, and the **system tray**.
- **Media keys** on the keyboard or a headset, and the system's own controls (Windows' media flyout; MPRIS on Linux).
- **Start with Windows / Linux** (Settings › General), **Discord** status, and **keyboard shortcuts** (Space, ←/→, W for Wrapped, E for the equalizer, / to search) with right-click menus everywhere.
- **Linux:** a .deb that brings mpv and ffmpeg. On desktops with no tray (Hyprland, Sway…) closing hides the window while music plays. If the window flickers or stays black, start it once with RADON_RENDER=software.

## Help

- **Favorite settings:** star (☆) the settings you use most and they sit together at the top of Settings.
- **Size of everything** (Settings › General): the whole app bigger or smaller, 80% to 250%; each device keeps its own.
- **Concert dates** on an artist's page, with a free Ticketmaster key of your own; add your city for shows near you.
- **Wake-up timer:** start a playlist at a time you set, while the app is open.
- **Updates:** checked at each start; **Update** in Settings installs it, and a card says what's new.
- **Problems** (Settings) lists anything that went wrong lately, and **Details for support** copies what someone helping you would ask for (no sign-ins or song names), with Clear for when it's fixed.
- **Clear unimportant cache and data** (Settings › General): shows the leftovers it found and their sizes, and clears them once you say so: pieces of downloads cut short, covers copied out of songs played before, an installed update, short-lived files (the DJ's last line, a sync's or an import's copy, pictures you shared), cut-short downloads of the DJ's voice or the listening model, copies of songs left by a tag change cut short, and the sign-in pages' web cache. Only files untouched for an hour. Settings, playlists, favorites, stats, equalizers and presets, lyrics and the lyrics scan, moods, sign-ins, backups, the DJ's voice, the listening model and the caches that make the app fast all stay.
- **Changelog and Features** are in the ☰ menu.
