# Changelog

What's new in each update of Radon Player, the newest at the top, from 4.0 on. Everything the app does is in **Features**; what the versions before 4.0 changed is kept on GitHub (CHANGELOG.md).

## 4.0.32 · November 2026
- **A tag write can't shorten or damage a song:** the lyrics and moods scans rewrote an MP3 whole, and two of them on the same file at once could write over each other, leaving a song that kept its size and played for 10 to 30 seconds. Every tag and cover write of the app's (the scans, the Tagger, covers, repairs, on the phone and the PC) now takes its turn, one file at a time; the change is made on a copy, and a copy holding less music than the song had is thrown away, your file untouched. After a write the file is measured, and written again if not all of it landed.
- **Full check:** Storage and damaged files (Settings › Scanner › Your files) has a second button that reads every song file from start to end and checks the sound itself: a FLAC's frames by their checksums and its length against what it says it holds, an MP3's frames one after another (a stretch that isn't music is found, with about where), an Ogg's or Opus's pages by their checksums, a WAV's and an M4A's parts against the file. It takes a long time (it reads your whole library) and can be stopped; what it found so far is shown. An MP3 cut off cleanly at its very end, and damage inside a WAV's or M4A's sound, can't be told. The quick Check is as it was.
- **The quick Check finds more:** an MP3 or WAV that plays for far less time than the music it holds is listed as damaged.
- **Stutter, static and cut-outs in hi-res FLACs (the phone):** the decoder was given room for 32 KB of a song at a time, and a bigger piece was thrown away (Problems: "Buffer too small", then "timestamp discontinuity"). It now has room for the biggest piece a FLAC can have. If the file itself is damaged at that spot a click may remain there: Full check lists such a file.
- **Problems is for faults:** another app taking the sound, or the car or headphones switching off, stops the music as it should and is no longer written in Settings › Problems.

## 4.0.31 · November 2026
- **Download new copies of damaged files:** Storage and damaged files (Settings › Scanner › Your files) has **Download new copies** under its list of damaged files. A song the app downloaded is got again the way it first was, and its damaged file removed only once the new one is saved. A file of your own is got from Tidal in FLAC (Tidal connected), and the damaged file goes to the bin only after its new copy is here and you say yes. Playlists, favorites and plays stay with each song; a song that can't be found again is left as it is.
- **The check finds more:** a FLAC whose tags and cover no longer lead to its music (what a tag write that was cut short leaves) is listed as damaged. Its first bytes look right, so the check missed it before.
- **A tag write can't leave half a file:** writing tags, lyrics, moods or a cover into a song now writes a whole copy first, checks that the copy still holds the music, and only then puts it in the song's place. A write that is interrupted (the app closed, the phone switched off) is finished the next time the app starts.
- **A damaged FLAC is put right when it's played (the phone):** a FLAC that fails with "PARSING_CONTAINER_MALFORMED" is looked through for where its music starts and its tags rebuilt around it, then played. A copy of the file as it was is kept. A file too damaged for that is left as it is: Download new copies replaces it.

## 4.0.30 · November 2026
- **What counts as a skip:** pressing Next (the button, the headphones, the notification) with more than 15 seconds of the song left, and nothing else. Picking another song from a list, the app moving on by itself, and going back are not skips. Before, every change of song but Previous counted, so the songs you play most led Most skipped.
- **A clean slate for the app's own skips:** they are counted from 21 November 2026. Skips counted before are left in place and not counted (so a Sync can't bring them back); songs heard to the end are counted as ever.
- **Spotify's skips, more strictly:** only the forward button is a skip (Spotify's own mark also covers going back and picking another song). Import your Spotify zip once more and its skips are redone.
- **The Skips list is in the right order:** two counted songs that are one file of yours showed as two rows with one's count, so a row sorted as 100 skips said 1. They are one row, added up.
- **Where your music came from counts by where you took the song:** a song put in the queue from a playlist, an album, an artist's page or search counts for that place when it plays. Radio and ∞ count only the songs they themselves added. From now on.
- **Milestones are in All-time Wrapped only, and match your plays:** they are worked out from the same plays the rest of that Wrapped shows. A week's, a month's and a year's Wrapped have no Milestones page.
- **This app's own play history, out and in:** Settings › Accounts (with the imports) has Export and Import for what Radon Player counted itself: its plays (the fallback for when Last.fm isn't connected, and Recently played), the songs you skipped or heard out, and its listening diary. Export writes one file; Import on another device or another install of the app adds what that one hasn't got. Importing the same file twice adds nothing.
- **A song is not rewritten while it plays:** the scans that write tags by themselves (lyrics, moods, covers from folders) leave the song playing and the next one alone; a tag write puts the whole file down again, and a song opened at that moment read as broken ("Couldn't play: PARSING_CONTAINER_MALFORMED"). A file that reads as broken is also tried once more after two seconds.
- **Covers on Wrapped's Never skipped and Most skipped:** looked up for songs that aren't yours, as Most played's are.

## 4.0.29 · November 2026
- **The cause of music going silent while the seek bar moved (the phone):** found and fixed. When the sound output had to finish what it held (at a song's end, and in the middle of a song whenever a fraction of a second went missing from the decoder), two of the app's own sound steps, the resampler and the peak repair, gave it a little more silence every time they were asked, where they should have answered once. The output never finished, and was fed silence without end while its clock ran on, until the song was skipped. Both answer once now, so after such a gap the song carries on.
- **Android's own FLAC decoder (the phone):** Settings › Sound quality has a switch to decode FLACs with Android's own decoder in place of the phone maker's. The maker's was seen dropping a fraction of a second of a song now and then (the stutter). Off at first: turn it on if you still hear short skips, and off again if songs sound wrong with it.
- **Wrapped asks which one you want:** opening Wrapped shows This week, This month, This year and All time, and puts together only the one you tap. It used to start loading the year at once.
- **The time you picked is the one shown:** All time (or any other) loaded, then was replaced a moment later by the year that had been loading behind it. A result is shown only for the time still asked for.

## 4.0.28 · November 2026
- **Wrapped's summary card:** your top artists and top songs each show their plays, as your top obsessions show their repeats. On a phone held upright the three lists go one under another across the card's whole width, so every song, artist and count is shown whole.
- **Most skipped and Never skipped are top 15s.**
- **Your playlists are counted:** a play from one of your playlists was written down under the tab's name, so Wrapped said you had played none. It is the playlist's now, and the plays already written down are put right (a sitting is the playlist most of its songs are in).
- **A song picked on an artist's or album's page is not radio:** those pages start a radio after their list, and the songs you picked yourself were counted as radio. Past plays are put right too.
- **Searching and queueing counts as a search:** a song found by searching and put in the queue (or Play next) counts toward Most searched and toward Search in Where your music came from, as a song tapped does. From now on.
- **Skips from your Spotify history:** the extended streaming history says which plays you skipped and which you heard out. Import the zip once more and they are added to the Skips tab, to Wrapped's Most skipped and Never skipped, and to what the DJ knows. Plays you have already are not doubled.
- **An Apple Music library's undated counts in All-time Wrapped:** songs, artists, albums, plays and minutes. Nothing that needs a day can use them.
- **Every source in every count:** Wrapped's compared-with-before now counts your imports on both sides; the week, month and year lists and the listening times take the plays in this app when Last.fm isn't connected.
- **Another app taking the sound for a moment is not written to Problems:** voice typing, a call or a spoken direction makes the music wait and come back, as it should. It is kept in Details for support.

## 4.0.27 · November 2026
- **A stutter leaves a line in Settings › Problems (the phone):** the sound output running dry, being made again mid-song, the player jumping by itself, or another app taking the sound for a moment is noted there with the song, the place in it and what happened just before (once every 20 seconds at most). Before, these were only in Details for support, kept while the app ran.
- **The fault behind the music cutting out, caught at its first step (the phone):** the player sometimes moves on to the next piece of a song while the phone's sound output is still part-way through the last. The output then either refuses the new piece (the player stopped: silence with the seek bar moving) or miscounts (a stutter, a jump in the song). The app now sees that moment, has the output let go of the leftover few thousandths of a second, and carries on, before the output sees anything wrong. Noted in Settings › Problems each time, with how far it jumped.
- **A song that plays on with nothing reaching the sound output is started again in place:** four seconds of the output taking no music while the player says it plays (seen once after a recovery: forty seconds of nothing): a seek in place, then a full reload, three times a song at most.
- **The decoder is logged:** made, let go or failing, and one made again in the middle of a song is noted in Problems.
- **Wrapped's summary lists your top 15 obsessions:** beside your top artists and top songs, your most repeated songs, each with how often it was repeated (the count first, so it is never cut off).
- **Obsession of each month shows the artist and the repeats:** under each month's song, whose it is and how many times it was repeated that month.
- **Wrapped's minutes and the listening times were far too big with an Apple Music history:** a day's count in it carries the whole day where a play has its length, and that was added up as listening (all-time Wrapped said 14 million minutes). Those plays count the song's own length now. Most played's listening times are added up again.
- **Obsessions are named as your own files are:** a song your history has under somebody else's name shows its real artist in Wrapped's repeats pages.

## 4.0.26 · November 2026
- **Music that went silent partway while the seek bar kept moving (the phone):** the player itself was stopping: the phone's sound output refused a piece of the song it was handed (Settings › Problems showed FAILED_RUNTIME_CHECK), and the app's clock counted on with nothing playing, again each time the song was loaded. The app now makes the output let go of what it was holding and hands the music in again, so the song carries on. Each time is noted in Settings › Problems.
- **A song that still won't keep playing moves on:** after being loaded again twice it goes on to the next song (or shows as paused, with nothing after it), not silence until you skip it.
- **It stays the phone's music player while it recovers:** a stopped song about to be loaded again no longer makes Android let the app go, after which it was refused ("startForegroundService() not allowed") with the screen off.
- **Every song is watched for going silent:** the watch for a song playing on with no sound only looked at hi-res music, and not at all with the equalizer doing nothing. MP3s, streams and CD-quality files are watched too, and started again in place.
- **Details for support says more about the sound:** what the sound output did lately (set up, let go, ran dry, failed, another app taking the sound) and when real sound last left the app.
- **The song the DJ names plays first:** a set's line was written before its songs were put in order, so a song said to lead could come second. The first song the line names is moved to the front.
- **Duplicates finds a download saved with no length:** a song downloaded from a list without lengths (Repeats, your history) showed no time and could not be matched to your other file of it. Its length is read from the file.

## 4.0.25 · November 2026
- **Your file under another of its artists:** a phone reads only the first artist of a file that credits two, so a song your history has under the other one showed as a stream in Repeats, and downloading it made a second copy. The song's length is now taken from your imported Spotify history as well as Apple Music's, and your only song of that name and that length is taken to be it.
- **Duplicates finds one song tagged two ways:** your files of the same name by an artist both credit, or of the same name and the very same length whoever each names, are listed together. A group put together by name and length alone has no copy marked Remove until you mark one, with a note to play both first: two different songs of one name can run to the same length.

## 4.0.24 · November 2026
- **Hide from stats:** long-press (right-click on a computer) a song, an album or an artist in a stats list and pick Hide from stats. It is no longer shown in Most played (songs, albums, artists and their listening times), Repeats, Skips or Unplayed and neglected; its plays still count in those lists' totals. Wrapped leaves it out altogether. Settings › Scanner › Last.fm & your stats › Hidden from stats lists what is hidden, with Show again. The list goes to your other device at a Sync.
- **Streaming or on this device:** every song in every list says where it plays from. A song of yours shows its format (or Local until the file has been read); a song that isn't on this device shows Streaming, with a small cloud on its cover where songs are shown as covers.
- **Covers found more often:** a song nothing was found for was not looked for again for two weeks. Every earlier miss is now looked for again once, a miss is tried again after three days, and a service that couldn't be reached after ten minutes. The search asks Deezer first and plainly, and Genius last, for the songs no shop sells.

## 4.0.23 · November 2026
- **A song your history has under somebody else's name is your file:** an unreleased song is often put up under an uploader's name, so your history had it under an artist who isn't on your file. Your only song of the same name and the same length (to the second) is now taken to be it: it shows with your file's artist, cover and tags, and its plays count for that artist in Most played, Artists and Wrapped. What plays from a service is never swapped this way.
- **This is a song of mine…:** for a song whose name is different too. A song's Options lets you pick which of your files it is, once (your songs of the same length are listed first); it is then shown, played and counted as that file. Your file's Options undoes it. A file with no artist in its tags is not accepted: give it one first.
- **WAVs keep their tags on the phone:** Android reads no tags from a WAV (its name came from the file, its album from the folder, its artist was unknown), so a WAV you tagged went back to Unknown artist at the next scan. The app now reads each WAV's own title, artist and album from the file.
- **Covers in Repeats, Recently played and Listen later:** a song that isn't yours and came with no picture has its cover looked up, as Most played does; when nothing has its cover, its artist's picture stands in.

## 4.0.22 · November 2026
- **Music that went silent partway through a song (the phone):** anything outside the app that talks to its player (the earbuds, a watch, the lock screen, the system) could tell that player to Stop, or to empty or replace what it held. The song then went silent while the app still took it for playing, and Play started it from its beginning. From outside, Stop is now a Pause and the song in the player is left alone. Each such request is noted in Settings › Problems.
- **Two safety nets for the same:** a player found idle with its song still meant to be playing is loaded again where it was (at most twice a song), and Play carries on from where the song was, not from its start. A volume still turned down after half a minute with the DJ not talking is put back to full, whatever was holding it.
- **No pop-up for what the DJ said, or for the equalizer changing with the output:** both are gone (the DJ is heard, and the Equalizer shows which one is on).
- **Messages are not played back late:** opening the app after a while showed every message made since, one after another. Only the newest waits, and one made more than a few seconds ago is not shown.
- **Details for support says more about the sound:** the output the music is on, the player's volume, and the last few things that changed (with their times), for finding what happened when music goes quiet.

## 4.0.21 · November 2026
- **The DJ has more than one way to say each thing:** the things it tells you between songs (about a song, an artist, your listening, your collection, your repeats) had one sentence each, so a set that came round again brought the same words. 74 of those lines now take turns with one or two others that say the same: 90 new wordings in all. None is said again until the others have had their turn, and it carries on from where it was after the app is closed. Introducing a set already had several wordings.
- **A song you used to repeat:** one of "You used to have this one on repeat, a long time ago. You haven't in a while.", "You wore this one out once. Not lately." and "This one used to be on repeat for you. It's been a while."

## 4.0.20 · November 2026
- **Other device's songs: the same song however each device names it:** songs both devices have were listed as missing when one of them was a file with no tags, because each device names such a file in its own way. They are now matched when one calls it "Artist - Title" and the other "Title" (or "Title - Artist"), with underscores in a name or without, with a track number in front ("11 Ruby") or not, with no artist on one side, and with a $ for an s ("$uicideboy$", "Suicideboys"). A song the other device really has and this one lacks is still listed.
- **A file with no artist, or named with its track number, is your song:** such a file ("11 Ruby", no artist) was not taken as your copy of a song named anywhere else (Repeats, Most played, your history), so it showed with no cover and as not on the device. It is now found without the number and, with no artist in its tags, by its name alone.
- **Linux: the close button on desktops with no tray and no minimized windows** (Hyprland, Sway and the like): the app answered Close by asking to be minimized, which those desktops don't do, so the button did nothing. There the window is now hidden while music plays (it goes on, the desktop's media controls still work, and opening the app again shows it) and the app closes when nothing is playing. A note says so the first time.
- **Size of everything is each device's own:** it went across at Sync, so the phone's size landed on the PC. It is no longer sent or taken by Sync, and a backup put on the other device leaves its size alone, so a device is at 100% until you change it there. Favorite settings, hidden choices and preset edits go across as before.
- **The DJ on a song you used to repeat:** "You used to have this one on repeat, a long time ago. You haven't in a while." (it named the month it was last on repeat).
- **Software drawing is gone from Settings:** it wasn't used. Where a window flickers or stays black, starting the app once with RADON_RENDER=software still draws it without the graphics card.

## 4.0.19 · November 2026
- **Your own songs are recognised in every list:** a list made from your history, or kept from before a song was downloaded, moved or tagged again, could show a song you have with no cover and as not on the device. A song is now found by what it is (the same download or file) first, then by its name and artist, then under any other artist it is credited to (history says "hard life & BENEE", your file says "BENEE": the same song), and is shown as it stands today: its cover (a WAV's too), length, format and tags. Repeats, its collage and the DJ's repeat sets use it for every song.
- **Now playing shows your file as it is:** a song played from your own copy out of such a list went on showing the old names, no cover and "streaming". The song playing is now your copy as it stands today.
- **Other device's songs works:** at each Sync the phone and the PC sent each other the list of their songs, and both dropped it on arrival, so Settings › Remote & sync › Other device's songs never had anything to show. It is kept now, and the first few missing songs are listed right under the setting (Review… has them all, to tick and download). Both devices need this version; then tap Sync now once.
- **Linux: Update works for a copy in your own folders:** on a Linux with no .deb installer (Arch, Fedora and the like) the app is unpacked into the home folder, and Update only downloaded a package nothing could install. Such a copy now updates itself: the new version is unpacked beside it, swapped in as the app closes and started, and the one before is kept once. A copy installed by the package manager updates as before.

## 4.0.18 · November 2026
- **Jump to a letter, bigger and on every long list:** the strip of letters is as tall as the list, wide enough for a thumb, and its letters are as big as fit (it was too small to read or hit on a phone); the rows keep clear of it. Every list of 20 or more has one now: songs anywhere, albums, artists, playlists, smart playlists, moods, pins, Most played's tabs, an artist's albums, New releases and Discovery. In a list in no order by name (by plays, by date, your own order) a letter goes to the first name that starts with it.
- **Size of everything (Settings › General):** makes the app's text and everything else bigger or smaller together, from 80% to 250%, for a screen where it all comes out too small or too big. On the PC it changes at once; on the phone the screen is drawn again. The PC's mini player keeps its usual size.
- **Play on the PC stops the phone's own song:** switching Play on (or pairing) left the phone's song playing under the PC's, with every button going to the PC. The phone now pauses what it was playing; its song and queue are kept for when you switch back.
- **Pinwheel visualizer:** it jumped about, more wildly the longer it had been running (the Fruit stand and Fairground presets use it). It now turns steadily, faster with the bass.
- **Linux, on Hyprland, Sway, niri, i3 and the like:** the app was drawn at the size it asked for in a corner of a bigger window, with a line round it. On those desktops it now starts itself with the setting that makes it follow its window. Other desktops and Windows are unchanged.

## 4.0.17 · November 2026
- **Lyrics card: tick the first line and the last:** the first line you tick is marked **1**; tick another and it is marked **2** and every line between them is ticked too (upward as well), so a verse is two taps. A ticked line unticks on its own, a third line starts another run, and **Clear** starts over. Still up to 25 lines; after a fill the card shows the lines in the song's order.
- **The Lyrics card box scrolls:** the list of lines keeps its full height under the card and the sliders (it had shrunk to two lines on a phone with the sound ticked). Scroll down past the card to reach the lines.
- **A clip is kept while its box is open:** once a clip is made (a lyrics card with sound, or Share a clip with sound) it goes to Instagram, then Facebook, then Save or Messages without being made again. Changing the clip makes a new one, and closing the box throws the kept one away (a saved copy stays).
- **Hear it and Start where it's playing start a paused song:** with the song paused they moved it without playing it.
- **Repeats from Apple Music:** Apple's Play Activity file often leaves the artist out of its rows, and those rows were all skipped, so no repeats were found from an Apple download that had the file. They are now kept for Repeats, the artist found by the song's title from the download's other files. **Import the zip again** to read them; your plays are not counted twice.
- **Jump to a letter on more lists:** the strip of letters now follows what a list is sorted by: songs sorted by artist or album get the artists' or albums' letters, a list from Z to A gets its strip the other way up, and playlists by name have one. A list in another order (by plays, by date) has none: letters would mean nothing there.
- **New releases: a bell, not a notification:** what the app's daily look finds (Settings › Playback › New releases by themselves) goes under a bell on the New releases screen, with a count of what is new; no notification is sent. It looks at the artists of the songs you have as files or downloads and of the songs in your Radon Player playlists; a playlist that is only on a streaming service doesn't count.
- **Unplayed and neglected, a new tab (Statistics):** the songs of yours you have never played, and the ones not played in 3 months, 6 months, a year or 2 years, the longest ago first, each with when it was last played and its plays; Play or Shuffle them from there. It goes by your stats (Last.fm and your imports) and the plays here.
- **Shuffle, three ways (Settings › Playback › Shuffle):** the Smart shuffle switch is now a choice. **Random** is pure chance; **Smart** is the old switch; **Unheard and neglected** plays only songs you haven't heard in a long time or at all, the never played first, then the ones heard longest ago, loosely shuffled. A song you have heard is left out of every shuffle, of any list, until the rest of that list has been heard too, then it goes round again; it remembers across queues and restarts, so a shuffled list can be shorter than the list it came from. A song you skip isn't counted as heard.
- **Mood lists is gone from the menu and the bar:** the Moods tab already opens each mood's songs. Nothing kept was deleted.

## 4.0.16 · November 2026
- **Select several and Hide, in every Personalization menu:** each menu (Look, Theme, Layout, Effects, the visualizer menus, the colour blends, Wrapped style, Lyrics card style, Presets) starts with **Select several**: tick as many as you like, then **Hide** them, **Copy codes**, or (presets) **Edit**. Hidden ones leave the menu and are listed under **Hidden** at its end: a tap brings one back, and "Show all of them again" brings them all. The one in use is never hidden while it is in use. Hide is also on each choice's own little menu (hold it, its ⋯, or right-click).
- **Edit the ready-made presets:** hold a ready-made preset (or tick several) and tap **Edit**: take any of its effects off (the others stay), add effects, and set Now playing's middle visualizer and its bottom one each to as it came, None, or another style. Its look, theme, layout and the rest stay as they came. Nothing ready-made is ever deleted; **Reset default preset edits**, at the end of every Personalization menu, puts them all back as they came. (Follows the cover and Glass cover take their effects and visualizers from each song, so they have none to edit.)
- **Share anything from Personalization as a code:** hold any choice in any Personalization menu and tap **Copy code** (or tick several and Copy codes): one line of text to send to someone or to your other device. Something you made goes as it is; a preset goes whole, with the look, layout, visualizers, colours, Wrapped style and lyrics card it uses when those are yours; a ready-made preset goes with your edits and arrives as a preset of theirs; any other ready-made choice goes by name and is put on when pasted. Settings › Personalization › **Share** has the box to paste a code into, and **Copy everything on now** and **Copy my effects**. Pasting adds to what you have, never replaces it. Until now only a look you had made yourself had a code, which is why most people saw only the paste box.
- **Clips with sound from streamed songs:** Share a clip with sound, and the lyrics card's With the song's sound, now work for a song you only stream: its sound is taken from the stream when you save or share (it needs the internet and takes a little longer), and its cover is fetched for the card. Tidal's hi-res comes in pieces a clip can't be cut from, so for the clip CD quality is asked for. A stream that only exists in pieces says it can't be clipped.
- **Sound on any lyrics card:** With the song's sound can be ticked for every song. A song whose words came from its own file (or the lyrics scanner) was shown without times, so the card said it had no timed lyrics: they are now looked up when you tick it. With timed lyrics the sound follows the lines you tick; **Starts** and **Ends** sliders are there to set it yourself (Back to the lines undoes that). When nobody has the lyrics timed, you set where the sound starts and ends.
- **Favorite settings:** the first section of Settings. Tap the ☆ beside any setting's name and it shows there too, for the ones you use all the time (Auto mono, say); it stays in its own section as well, and it is the same setting, not a copy. Tap ★ to take it out. Only the setting's own line shows there: extra rows some settings have under them stay in their section.
- **Fetch the next song ahead (Settings › Playback, on):** for songs you stream. While a song plays, the one after it in the queue is fetched whole into the app's cache and played from there, so it starts with no wait and can't stall halfway. Until now only its address was looked up ahead. Tidal's hi-res is put together as one FLAC, as a download is. Nothing is kept. It uses the same data as playing the song, plus a song's worth when you skip one that was fetched: switch it off on a small data plan. Not used while casting.
- **Repeats tab:** a song's line is its number alone ("#1 · 164"), so it is no longer cut short on a tile; and **Collage…** makes the grid of covers as on Most played, each with its repeats, for the time the tab shows.
