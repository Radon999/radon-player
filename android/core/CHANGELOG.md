# Changelog

What's new in each update of Radon Player, the newest at the top, from 4.0 on. Everything the app does is in **Features**; what the versions before 4.0 changed is kept on GitHub (CHANGELOG.md).

## 4.0.7 · November 2026
- **An artist's whole discography:** a release could be missing from an artist's page (an older EP, say). YouTube Music's artist page shows only the first few of each kind, and the app never asked for the rest: it does now. And the releases Genius lists for the artist that the service still leaves out are added, marked "on Genius"; open one and its songs play from your copy, or are looked for on Tidal and YouTube.
- **Singles that are on an album or EP already are folded away (streaming only):** on an artist's page a service's single whose song is on one of their albums or EPs is left out, so the song is found on its release and the list is shorter. In a service's search, a song found both as its single and on a release shows as the release's, and the single is not listed among the albums. An EP always stays, also when an album has its songs. Your own files are never touched by this: a single you have stays in your music. What was left out is under Show what's left out, and can be put back.
- **Every song of an artist shows by itself:** the Show every song button is gone. Every song is listed right under their albums and singles as soon as the page has gone through their releases, above your songs and their top songs. With Only my music picked, it shows only what you have, as before.
- **Only streaming, on an artist's page:** a third choice in the menu at the top, beside Everything and Only my music: what the services have, without your own files.
- **Sort an artist's page as the other tabs:** a Sort menu for their releases (newest, oldest, by name), for your songs (title, album, added, most and least played, last played, length, shuffled) and for Every song. Each is kept.
- **Genius search finds artists and releases too:** it used to list only songs. It now shows artists, then albums, EPs and singles, and a song by itself only when it was released by itself: a song on an album or EP shows as that release, which opens with all its songs. Tapping one of several same-named artists opens that name's page sorted with the one you tapped.
- **A song that went silent partway (phone):** the sound could stop a little way into a song while the song went on counting. If the equalizer's numbers ever run away (a band at or over half the song's sample rate could make them), they are set back at once and the music goes on, and a band can no longer be built that high. If it happens again, Settings › Problems now says exactly what turned the sound down.
- **Picking a song in the queue picks that one only:** with the same song waiting in the queue more than once, holding one of them picked every one. Each row of the queue is now its own: pick one and only it is ticked, and Remove from the queue takes out just the rows you picked.

## 4.0.6 · November 2026
- **Hold a song and picking starts:** holding a song (right-click on a computer) starts multi-select at once with that song picked, everywhere: lists, grids and the queue. Before, some places opened the song's menu and Select was at the bottom of it. A song's own menu is its ⋮; while picking, holding a song opens the options for what's picked.
- **Shuffle shuffles the whole list:** started halfway down a playlist, Shuffle used to mix only what was still to come, so the songs above never played. It now takes every song of the list, with the one playing first and the ones you queued next.
- **Move, don't duplicate (Settings › Playback):** a switch for what Play next and Add to queue do with a song already waiting in the queue. Off, as it always was: it's put in again. On: it's moved to where you put it, so it's in the queue once.
- **The app's messages get out of the way:** tap one, or swipe it to either side, and it's gone, so it never keeps you from Play, Pause or Skip.
- **An artist's page starts with their albums and singles,** then your songs, then their top songs; and your songs are sorted by album until you pick another sort (which is then kept).
- **Every song of an artist:** at the bottom of an artist's page, Show every song goes through all their releases (albums, EPs, singles, and the compilations they are on) and lists each song once. Their top songs are only the service's twenty most played, so a song that is not one of those was nowhere on the page.
- **Two bands with the same name:** when a service has more than one artist under a name, the page offers which one, each with a song it is known for. Your choice is kept, so the name means that band from then on, and the page shows only its songs and albums from the services. Your own files carry only the name, so those still show together.
- **A mixed-up artist sorted with Genius:** some services put two bands of one name under a single artist, songs and albums all mixed (it happens to small, little-known bands). When Genius has more than one artist of that name, the page offers **Sort with Genius**: pick the one you mean, and only the songs and releases on that artist's list on Genius stay. It is a separate switch from the service's own "which one?": use either, or both, or turn it off. Show every song then also lists the songs only Genius knows, each once (Genius's separate pages for the album's, the single's and the EP's version of a song count as one).
- **Hide songs and albums from an artist's page yourself:** for when both the service and Genius get it wrong. A song's Options (or an album's menu) has "Hide from this artist's page"; pick several songs first to hide them together. Nothing is deleted: **Show what's left out** at the end of the page lists everything hidden or left out by Genius, and "Show on this artist's page" puts one back, whatever Genius says. Your own files are never left out unless you hide them.
- **Search Genius:** a new place in the search bar's menu. Genius lists nearly every song, so one that no service has can still be found by name. It has no music itself: a song you have plays from your copy, any other is looked for on Tidal and YouTube when you play it.
- **Streamed songs have their covers in Android Auto:** the cover of a song that streams was handed to the car in a way it could not open, so only downloaded songs and your own files showed one. It is also fetched before the song starts now, so it is there the first time.
- **The music no longer stops when the sound moves between your earbuds and the car (phone):** with both connected, Android tells the app the same thing as when headphones are unplugged, and the song paused partway. It still pauses for a moment, then goes on by itself when something is still connected to play through. Unplugging your only headphones pauses as before.

## 4.0.5 · November 2026
- **Checking for an update always finishes:** Settings could stay on "checking for a newer version…" for good. The check waited its turn among the app's other background work (all of it busy during a scan or a sync) and had no time limit of its own. It now runs by itself, ends within about a minute whatever the network does, and has a second way to find the newest version when GitHub won't give its list. Downloading the update no longer waits for other work either.

## 4.0.4 · November 2026
- **A song you have is never downloaded twice:** if you already have a song with the same title and artist (whatever its album is tagged as), Download leaves it and says so: your copy is the one that plays. Before, a download whose tags you changed (a single moved into its album) could be taken for a song you didn't have, streamed instead of played from your file, and downloaded again as a second copy. "Keep both" (Settings, or when asked about a near match) still downloads it.
- **A download follows its file:** when a downloaded song's file has moved (after a tag change put it in another folder), the app finds your file of that song again and goes on playing it, instead of streaming it.

## 4.0.3 · November 2026
- **The visualizer works for streamed songs (computer):** it stood still for any song that was streaming rather than playing from a file on that computer (a hi-res stream, or a song you only have on the phone). It listens to the stream now.
- **Pick an artist's or an album's songs whole, in Search:** hold an artist or an album in the search results (right-click on a computer) and all its songs are picked, as songs are; while picking, a tap does the same and a tick shows it. Then play, queue, download, tag or delete them together. In your music it's every song you have of them; on a service, an album's songs and the songs an artist's page lists.
- **The right bit depth under Now playing (computer):** a 24-bit FLAC was shown as "32-bit" (the player decodes it into 32-bit numbers, and that was what the app showed). It shows the file's own depth now, and none for MP3 and AAC, which have no bit depth. The 32-bit FLACs tab was right all along.
- **A USB DAC is recognised as the output (Windows):** the app worked out Windows' default output from when a device was last picked by hand, so a DAC Windows chose as it was plugged in (a FiiO KA17) wasn't seen: the Equalizer showed another device and used its settings. The app now asks Windows itself. The music always went to the right device; only the app's idea of it was wrong.

## 4.0.2 · November 2026
- **A Q button for your queues:** at the top left of Now playing, where "From the album…" stood. Beside the other buttons there was only room for "F…". Q opens your queues just as those words did.

## 4.0.1 · November 2026
- **Moods match (Settings › Remote & sync):** a new switch under Tags match, for moods only, with its own main device. At each sync the other device's songs get the main one's moods, written into their files, with nothing scanned again. For when one device's moods were lost or cleared, or when you scan on one and tag on the other.
- **Tags match leaves moods alone:** it used to make its main device lead the moods too. Now the tags and the moods are apart: each follows the device you pick for it.
- **Moods only sync when you ask:** a sync used to carry moods both ways by itself. Now they go across only with Moods match on. Turn it on if you want your two devices' moods kept the same.
- **Tidal's sign-in page opens by itself (computer),** as its text said it did.
- **The music stops after four songs in a row that can't be played** (no connection, a service turning the app away), and says why, instead of skipping on through the whole queue.
- **Linux:** links open in your browser (sign-ins, the update's page, folders); a link that can't be opened is copied instead. With no tray, closing the window minimizes it instead of hiding it for good. Software drawing (Settings › General) for a window that flickers.

## 4.0.0 · November 2026
- **Rebuilt from the inside:** the app was taken apart and put back together as separate parts (playback, the DJ, your library, downloads, the stats, the sound), each with its own tests. It does what it did, more smoothly and more reliably, and your settings, stats, playlists and presets carry over.
- **Linux:** Radon Player now runs on Linux as well as Windows and Android, as a .deb package that brings mpv and ffmpeg with it. It has the tray, Start with Linux, updates from inside the app, and the keyboard's and a headset's media keys with the desktop's own "now playing" controls.
- **Music no longer stops at the end of a song (phone):** with the DJ on, a song heard to its end could leave a long silence before the next. The next song now comes at once.
- **Bit-perfect keeps every bit:** a 24-bit song always reaches a USB DAC as 24-bit (some were brought down to 16-bit first).
- **The DJ's voice is the same everywhere:** one natural voice on the phone and the computer, fetched once from DJ › Voice settings. She no longer says things a voice can't say well (dots for pauses, signs), and places in a row are said in words.
- **Mood sets of their own:** Happy, Chill, Calm, Sad and Dark each have their own DJ set; only Energetic, Party and Hype share one.
- **Moods read better:** the sound is judged on a scale that fits real records, the words count for more, and a song gets four moods at most. Reset moods (Settings) and Scan moods put the new ones in your files.
- **The DJ's facts take turns:** what it tells you goes round by subject, and it knows more about your collection.
- **Smart shuffle knows more:** "haven't heard in a while" now goes by your plays everywhere (Last.fm and your imports), not only the plays in the app.
- **Download from the notification (phone):** with the Shuffle button switched off in Settings, a streamed song you don't have shows a Download button in the notification and on the lock screen.
- **Refresh in every tab,** an Unreleased tab, covers read from a song's folder, singles and EPs sorted by their tag, and Tidal asked again before YouTube is used.
- **Asked for at the start (phone):** everything the app needs is asked for as it starts, so the visualizer and the rest work the first time.
