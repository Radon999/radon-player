# Changelog

What's new in each update of Radon Player, the newest at the top. Everything the app does is in **Features**.

## 3.2.2 · November 2026
- **Each device keeps its own look:** Sync no longer copies the look, theme, layout, visualizer choices or picked effects between the phone and the PC, so each can look different. The looks, layouts and visualizers you made still go to both: one made on the phone is on the PC after the next sync.
- **Lyrics cards in your look:** a lyrics card now takes the look in use on that device: its background (and fade), its stars and effects, the lyrics on a rounded card like the Lyrics screen's (with a small LYRICS heading) and the song's name in its accent, with your own file's cover too. The Now playing look still uses the cover's colours.
- **How long a mood scan takes:** Scan moods, Full scan, Read moods from lyrics, Rescan and writing into files each say about how long they'll take before you start (from how fast they went last time on this device), and while running they show the time left right beside how far they've got.
- **Hold on the phone:** hold your finger on any look, theme, layout, effect or visualizer in its menu (or a style in the Visualizer tab): Favourite for every one, and Edit and Delete too for the ones you made, shown right under it, as right-click does on the PC. After a favourite moves to the top, holding or tapping always acts on the one under your finger. Each one also has a ⋯ at its right that opens the same choices with a tap.
- **Wrapped's top singles and EPs fit:** a full top 15 shows as one list, like the top songs, so all of it fits on the phone's screen.
- **Edit from the menu:** right-click (or hold) one of your own looks, layouts or visualizers in its menu and tap Edit: its maker opens with every setting it has, name included, ready to change.
- **Tag any song on the phone:** the Tagger can now change your own songs' tags and covers on the phone too (FLAC, MP3, M4A, Ogg, WAV and more, each in its own format), not just the app's downloads. Android asks once: tap Allow in the Tagger and turn on All files access for Radon Player.
- **Your own looks keep their effects:** the effects picked in Settings › Personalization › Effects now go with the ready-made looks only; a look you made shows the effects you gave it.

## 3.2.1 · November 2026
- **Picture menus stay open on the phone:** in Look, Theme, Layout, Now playing visualizer and Effects, a tap puts that one on and shows its picture right under it, and the menu stays open so you can try others. Back or a tap outside closes it. (Layout shows its picture as you tap and goes on when you close the menu, since a new layout redraws the whole screen.)
- **Repeat one never gets stuck at the end:** a song whose player ran on past its end (or sat at -0:00) without saying it had finished now starts again on Repeat one, or moves on to the next song otherwise, within a second or two, on the phone and the PC.
- **Visualizers keep moving on the phone:** every visualizer on screen (Now playing's, the Visualizer tab's, Make your own visualizer's) now shares one listener to the music, so one no longer freezes another; it follows each new song and starts listening again by itself if it goes quiet while music plays.
- **Previews in a small window:** on the PC, with the window small and the menu at the right edge, a picture no longer flickers: it goes beside the menu where there's room, else under or over the one pointed at, never under the pointer.
- **Your own looks, layouts and visualizers on every device:** Sync now puts together what you made on the phone and on the PC (each one kept, not one device's list swapping out the other's), and one you delete goes on both. Restoring a backup adds its looks, layouts and visualizers beside the ones you made on that device, on any device.
- **Dual visualizers:** Make your own visualizer has a Dual switch. Start from greys out and two rows appear: one of the styles drawn in the middle and one of the styles along the bottom. Both play at once; on Now playing the middle one goes over the cover and the other along the bottom.
- **Your own colours for every effect:** Pick an effect (in Make your own look, or Personalization › Effects) and its colour shows below; tap it to change its colour, or Look's colours to put it back.
- **Randomize colours:** Make your own look, the colour blends and the effects' colours each have a Randomize colours button that changes only the colours, as a start to build on.
- **Rain and snow over everything:** The Rain and Snow effects now fall over the seek bar and buttons too (taps still reach them).
- **Favourites and right-click:** Right-click (or hold your finger on) a look, layout or visualizer in its menu: ★ Favourite puts it at the top of the list, and the ones you made can be deleted from there.
- **Saved colours:** Make your own look and the colour blends have Save these colours and a Saved colours menu: your saved sets and 15 ready-made four-colour sets in each. Favourite any of them (★, to the top); yours can be deleted, the ready-made ones stay.
- **Test the key always answers:** It says whether the key worked or not every time, even when Anthropic doesn't answer (after 25 seconds).
- **Full screen visualizers on Now playing:** Make your own visualizer has a Full screen switch: Now playing then shows your visualizer over the whole screen, cover and all, just as the Visualizer tab draws it (the buttons still work under it), instead of around the cover or along the bottom. The maker's preview shows where the cover sits.
- **Test the key shows its answer:** In Lyrics & moods, Test the key now says right under the button whether the key works (and tests the key in the box, even before Save), so the answer can't hide behind the settings.
- **Randomize in every maker:** Make your own look, layout and visualizer each have a Randomize button that sets every choice at random (colours, fade, glow, stars and effects; the way round, the bar's buttons, the mini player, Now playing and every More choice; the style and its shaping, or a colour blend), as a start you can change.
- **Every layout choice shows in its picture:** Make your own layout's picture now shows the More choices too: the rail's side, a floating bar, the bar's background, how the screen you're on is marked, names, icon and title sizes, the cover's shape, size and shadow, the seek bar's thickness, its times and swiping the cover.
- **Colour blends on your own style:** in Make your own visualizer, the colour blend's preview draws the style you're making (from the style tab), not the Visualizer's style, so you see both together as you change the colours.
- **The DJ's natural voice on the phone too, if you want it:** the phone keeps its own voice unless you get the PC's human-sounding one in DJ › Voice settings (about 130 MB, once; no internet needed after). Then you can pick either one there, or remove the download again.
- **Update check at start:** each time the app opens it checks for an update and tells you whether there is one or not.
- **Wrapped's top 15s fit on one screen:** the top songs, albums, EPs, singles and discoveries no longer run under the phone's buttons at the bottom.
- **Your moods go everywhere:** every mood a song has (not just its main one) is saved in its file's MOOD tag, and Sync and backups carry them all, song by song, so your other device or a restored one has them without a scan.
- **Every mood you picked comes across:** a song you gave several moods in the Tagger keeps all of them on your other device after a sync or a restore (it used to keep only the first).
- **Personalization tidied:** the old "Fallback for Now playing look" line is gone.
- **Wrapped's top singles and EPs checked:** a release whose tag calls it a single when it's really an EP (or the other way round) is checked against Deezer or Apple Music (once, then remembered) and shown with the right ones; Settings › Albums, EPs and singles can fix the tags themselves.
- **Make your own visualizer's preview bigger again,** with a little more room under it, so the whole visualizer shows.

## 3.2.0 · November 2026
- **Rescan moods (Moods tab):** for when moods or mood stats are lost or spoilt. Every song is heard again and its words read again (by the AI when Read with AI is on), and the new moods replace the ones the last scan wrote into your files. Moods you picked yourself stay.
- **Moods always take a best guess:** when a song's sound and words disagree (happy-sounding, sad words), it can now be both, and a song the scan is unsure of still gets its likeliest mood instead of none. Fix any that are off in the Tagger as you hear them.
- **Effects with any look (Settings › Personalization):** turn off Look's own effects and pick any of the effects, stars included, from a menu, with any look or theme.
- **More pictures:** Now playing visualizer's menu shows each style moving on Now playing; a look's picture shows your Now playing visualizer in that look's colour; Make your own layout and Make your own look show your screens big at the top, changing as you go.
- **Previews beside the list:** on the PC, a look's, theme's or layout's picture now pops out to the left of the one the pointer is over, level with it, so it's always in sight however far down the menu you scroll.
- **Bigger, truer previews:** a look's picture is a small Now playing in that look (the cover, the song's name, the seek bar and buttons in its colour, and the visualizer in its own colour); themes show more of a list; layouts are drawn bigger.
- **A bigger visualizer in Make your own visualizer,** so it's easier to see what you're making.
- **Long menus fit the screen:** on the PC, a menu too long for the window (like Now playing's visualizer styles) opens in more columns side by side, growing to the left, with a line between them, instead of running off the bottom.
- **Deezer import says why when the file is right but empty:** if Deezer's file only has a play or two in its listening history (or only short ones), it now says so plainly instead of a list of sheets.
- **Covers in the car again:** Android Auto couldn't open the covers the app keeps on the phone, so it showed a blank square; now it's handed them in a way it can read.
- **Smoother with look effects:** moving effects (stars, rain, aurora and the rest) redraw without making the screen around them work again each frame.

## 3.1.0 · November 2026
- **15 layouts** (Settings › Personalization › Layout): a menu, a tab bar, a side rail or tabs along the top; the ☰ menu on the left or right; the mini player floating, as a bar, a pill or at the top; and six ways to lay out Now playing. Every feature can be reached from all of them.
- **Make your own:** a **layout** from slots you pick and order, a **look** with your own colours (with a neon glow and stars if you like) and a **visualizer** with its own style and colour blend, all previewed live as you make them.
- **SoundCloud (streaming only):** search, your likes and playlists, artists and radio. SoundCloud is its own section: its songs can't be downloaded or mixed into playlists and queues with other music. Spotify, Deezer, YouTube, Tidal and Apple Music can still be mixed with your offline music.
- **Now playing in your look:** the look's background (its gradient and stars) shows behind Now playing, and the song name, buttons, seek bar, volume, tools, visualizer and lyrics are in the look's colour, made brighter or darker when needed so they always stand out from the background.
- **The DJ in the queue:** while the DJ plays, the queue shows what it said, the set's category (on each song too) and any stat it gave, as on the DJ tab.
- **Cover blur is gone:** Now playing's background is always plain.
- **Tags & covers match (Settings › Remote & sync):** pick the main device, the PC or the phone, and at each sync the songs on both get its tags and album art on the other. The main device always sends, the other always takes.
- **Deezer history import fixed:** it now finds the listening history in any of the spreadsheet's sheets, however it's written (dates and columns in more forms, the zip as it comes, or a .csv), and reads it on the PC as on the phone.
- **The DJ never repeats a song for 20 sets:** another song of the same mood or kind of set takes its place, and this is remembered after the app closes.
- **Previews in Personalization:** every look, theme and layout has a picture and a description in its menu (point at one on the PC to see it big).
- **31 effects for your own look** (aurora, synthwave grid, rain, snow, fireflies, a city skyline, a neon frame and more), any number at once, with every theme and layout.
- **More choices in Make your own layout:** the rail's side, a floating bar, the bar's background, how the current screen is marked, icon and title sizes, no mini player, the cover's shape, size and shadow, the seek bar and its times, and swiping the cover.
- **Test your visualizer with a song:** search your music or a service right in Make your own visualizer and play it while you shape it.
- **Every import says how it went:** Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer, YouTube Music and Tidal history and playlist imports show whether the last one worked and what it added, or why it failed, under their line. Deezer's says which sheets and columns it found and why rows didn't count.
- **Sync keeps your moods:** a sync no longer swaps a song's moods for the other device's just because that device read the words differently, so mood playlists don't lose songs. With Tags & covers match on, the main device's moods lead: it never takes the other's. Undo last sync now puts the moods back too.
- **Glow visualizer in your look's colour:** on the look's colour it's now shades of the look's accent (it was always blue to violet).
- **The visualizer has its own colour for each look:** on Now playing, with any look, the visualizer is a colour that sets off the look's accent (a purple look gets a warm yellow, a blue one an amber), so it goes with the look but is never the same colour as the song name, buttons and seek bar.
- **Moods spoilt by a sync can be fixed:** the Moods tab offers to hear again the songs whose moods came from the other device or a backup.
- **Sync never touches your MP3s and M4As on the phone:** a sync could write FLAC tags into them, which broke them (they wouldn't play). Only FLACs the app downloaded can now have their tags or cover changed on the phone, and nothing but a FLAC is ever rewritten as one. Songs are only matched when both lengths are known and agree, and a cover only goes to the same album.
- **Folders sync leaves alone** (Settings › Remote & sync, which was Play on): put unreleased music there and a sync never changes its tags or covers.
- **Every mood goes into the file:** a song's MOOD tag now holds all its moods (up to five, its main one first), not just the main one, so your other device and other apps see the same moods and mood lists don't shrink there. Write moods into the files once more to update them.
- **Tags & covers match only changes what's missing or wrong:** a tag that already says the same thing (another case or spacing, "A, B" and "A; B", a year and a full date) and a cover that's already the same picture are left exactly as they are.
- **Sync only when you ask:** the phone and PC no longer sync by themselves when they meet; only Sync now (on either) syncs.

## 3.0.1 · November 2, 2026
- **Downloads go to Music › Artists:** every download is saved in its artist's own folder there (Artists / Sody / Singles / Let Go - Single). If your download folder was plain Music, it's moved to Music › Artists once; Settings › Downloads can pick another.
- **Files follow their tags:** change a download's artist or album in the Tagger and its file moves to that artist's and album's folder (made if it isn't there), and the folder it left goes once no music is left in it. Your own files move too when they were in the folder their old tags gave them; folders you arranged yourself stay as they are.
- **Editing the playing song:** tag changes to the song that's playing are no longer dropped: they're saved the moment it ends.
- **The phone app's file is now RadonPlayer.apk** (OmarchyMusic.apk is still there for phones on 3.0.0 to update).
