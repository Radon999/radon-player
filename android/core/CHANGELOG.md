# Changelog

What's new in each update of Radon Player, the newest at the top, from 4.0 on. Everything the app does is in **Features**; what the versions before 4.0 changed is kept on GitHub (CHANGELOG.md).

## 5.2.9 · November 2026
- **Favorite artists for New releases (the phone and the PC):** a shorter list of the artists you really want to hear about, beside everyone you follow (who all stay in the whole list). **★ Favorites**, next to Refresh… and Select (and on the PC widget), shows only theirs, and the bell in it only their new releases, with a count of its own. Add or remove one from a release's menu (right-click or hold it), several picked at once, or in Utilities › Followed artists.
- **Followed artists, more (the phone and the PC):** pick several (Select) and **Add to favorites** or **Remove from favorites**; **★ Favorites only** shows just them; a favorite says **★ New releases looked for and prioritized**. It's a grid too (the page's layout picker): pictures and names, a ★ by the favorites.
- **The ☰ menu, tidied (the phone and the PC):** new dropdowns **Categories** (Genres, Moods), **Streaming** (the library of the service picked) and **Discography diving** (Discovery, Listen later); New releases, Followed artists, Folders, Unreleased, the Equalizer and the Mini player are under **Utilities**.
- **Edit the lyrics from the Lyrics widget:** on the PC, **Edit** in the widget (or **Write lyrics** when none were found) opens them right there, with their times if they're timed; **Save** puts them into the song's file (a streamed song's are kept in the app). On the phone a home screen widget can't take typing, so its new ✎ button opens the app straight in the Lyrics tab's Edit.
- **↑ Top in Now playing's queue (the phone and the PC, and their widgets):** scrolled down the queue, a **↑ Top** button takes you back to the top. On the phone's home screen widget it's always there while the queue shows (a home screen widget can't tell how far down it is). The widgets also show **the whole queue** now (it stopped at 100 songs on the phone and 300 on the PC) and **every new release** in the period picked (it stopped at 15 on the phone and 60 on the PC), to scroll through as in the app.

## 5.2.8 · November 2026
- **Follow artists for New releases (the phone and the PC):** New releases now looks only for the artists you follow. With **Follow artists by themselves** on (Settings › General, on at first), the artists of the songs on your device and of your Radon Player playlists are followed as they come; a streaming service's own playlists (Tidal's) never count. Follow anyone from their menu (right-click or hold an artist), or from a song's options for several artists at once, so artists you only stream can be followed too.
- **Unfollow and hide (the phone and the PC):** unfollow from an artist's menu, from a song's options, or **Hide** an artist right in New releases (one, or the releases you picked). **Utilities › Followed artists** lists everyone you follow, to unfollow one or several. An unfollow always stays. Switching following by themselves off unfollows no one; switched on again, it follows the artists added meanwhile.
- **Follow or unfollow all your artists at once (the phone and the PC):** under the switch, **Follow all my artists** and **Unfollow all my artists** do every artist of your songs and Radon Player playlists in one go.
- **Picking in the New releases widget (the PC):** Select… and ctrl- or shift-click now tick releases in the widget itself (never in the app's window), as Most played and Up next do.
- **The right artist of a name in New releases (the phone and the PC):** two artists can share a name. The one whose releases share titles with your own songs by that name is the one looked at now, so a stranger of the same name (another Forrest) doesn't show, and hiding him can't hide yours. If one still slips through, **Not my …** on the release says that artist is someone else: their releases go for good and yours of that name stays followed.
- **Hide Deezer-only (New releases and its widget):** one switch shows only the releases you can play (Tidal, YouTube Music); off, the ones only on Deezer show too, so you know something new came out.
- **New releases the same in the app and on both widgets:** the tab, the PC widget and the phone widget all say where each release plays from, follow Hide Deezer-only and say why a release can't play; Not my … and Hide the artist are in the tab and on the PC widget (a phone widget opens the release in the app, where they are).
- **✕ on the options list (the phone and the PC):** the list of a song's options (Add to favorites, Add to a playlist…) has ✕ at its top right to close it, in the app and in the widgets, for a small window where there's nowhere else to click.
- **Full screen on the PC's widgets:** each widget's full screen button opens its own place in the app, in front: the Visualizer tab, the DJ, Most played, New releases, Now playing's lyrics of the song playing, and so on. (The Lyrics widget's Open in the app is gone: full screen does it.)

## 5.2.7 · November 2026
- **Volume on every widget that plays (the phone and the PC):** Now playing, Now playing + playlists, Lyrics and Visualizer have the volume with play/pause: a slider on the PC, a bar with − and + on the phone (a home screen widget can't be dragged).
- **Songs in the PC's widgets, as in the app:** right-click a song in Up next, Most played or New releases for the app's own options; ctrl- or shift-click to pick several. A song's artist and album, and a new release's artist and Single/EP/Album, open their pages in the app.
- **Where each new release plays from (the phone and the PC):** new releases are found on Deezer, then looked for on Tidal first and YouTube Music next, whatever service you browse with. Each one says which (Tidal, YouTube Music, or "Deezer only: can't play yet"), in New releases and on its widget.
- **Why a song can't play, said (the PC):** a widget now says at its foot what it's doing and, if a song can't play, why (only on Deezer so far, Tidal not signed in, no connection). The newest releases are looked up ahead, so they start at once.
- **Periods on the widgets (the phone and the PC):** Most played (week, month, year, all time) and New releases (week, month, year; and albums, EPs or singles on the PC) choose right on the widget.
- **Widgets stay out of the taskbar (Windows):** only Radon Player itself is in the taskbar and Alt+Tab, widgets layer like any other window, and a widget's Lyrics card (or anything it opens in the app) brings the app's window to the front.
- **Widget settings kept and synced:** the widgets' choices (Widgets in the app's look, the Visualizer widget's) now sync between your phone and PC, and Settings says which look the widgets copy (with the Now playing look, that's the cover's colours).

## 5.2.6 · November 2026

It works whatever version is running, and even when you click twice quickly. Versions before this one couldn't always tell, so a second copy could open.

Once you move either, Size of Now playing switches itself off, since those parts are sized on their own; put both back to 100% and it's on again, as it was. Each device keeps its own sizes.

## 5.2.5 · November 2026

The visualizers over, around and along the bottom of the cover change with it; full screen ones still fill the screen. The rest of the app stays as Size of everything has it, and each device keeps its own size.

## 5.2.4 · November 2026
- **The Visualizer widget follows Now playing (the phone and the PC):** it now shows the visualizer you picked for Now playing (with Dual, its bottom one too) and changes the moment you change it there, instead of always being bars; on the phone it's drawn in full colour, the way the app draws it. With Now playing's visualizer Off it shows the Visualizer tab's. In **Settings › Personalization › Widgets** you can instead give it the Visualizer tab's style or one of its own, and a colour of its own.

The download-or-copy setting is gone. A song you already have here (as a file or a download, however the other device names it) is no longer listed, and each row says **On your phone** / **On your PC** instead of Streaming.
- **Discord shows your music again (the PC):** 5.2.3 made Always on top ("Competing in") the way it shows at first, and some Discords then showed nothing. **Listening to** is the first choice again, and if your Discord won't take the kind you pick, it switches to Listening to by itself and Settings › Discord tells you.

A dropped connection carries on where it stopped, and each file is checked before it's saved. Both apps need to be open, as for Sync now; a song the PC asks for is sent by the phone the next time it checks in.
- **PC widgets that show up and don't slow anything (Linux):** on Linux the widgets never appeared and slowed the whole app down (they were see-through windows the graphics card couldn't show). They're now plain cards the desktop rounds itself, they open where you left them and at their size (on Hyprland too), and the app stays smooth. **Always on top** is off unless you turn it on, so other windows go over the widgets.
- **Shortcuts with plain names and their own icons (the PC):** "Wrapped all time", "DJ with only my songs" or the playlist's name, each with an icon that shows what it does (a playlist's own cover). A new **Shortcuts** widget puts the ones you made on your screen as one-click buttons, for desktops without desktop icons (Hyprland and the like), where the app no longer puts files that only opened a text editor. **Add to the taskbar** (a pin next to each shortcut) puts it on Omarchy's taskbar or GNOME's dash after your own pins; on Windows, **Open the shortcuts folder** and pin one by hand (right-click › Pin to taskbar).
- **Phone widgets polished:** they keep up with the music even when the app was started from a widget or headphones without its window, keep rounded corners in the cover's colour on every Android version, stay readable on bright covers, never cut text off at their smallest sizes, and Play, Next and Previous start the music without opening the app. The queue on Now playing stays right after you reorder it, and while the phone is the PC's remote the widgets show the PC's song. The Visualizer only moves while the phone is unlocked (and rests after ten minutes); Most played no longer wakes the phone every hour.

## 5.1 · November 2026
- **Queued clean/explicit changes (Utilities, the phone and the PC):** songs got from another service or app (DumpMedia mostly) often come as the clean version when you wanted explicit, or the other way round. Select them anywhere and pick **Queue a clean/explicit change**: they go on a list in Utilities › Queued clean/explicit changes. Pick **Change to: Explicit** or **Clean** beside **Scan**, and the scan reads each song's name, artist, album and length and finds that version on Tidal. **▶ Found version** plays it to check, and **Replace** (or **Replace all found**) downloads it, then deletes the version it replaces: a download of the app's straight away, your own file into the trash (on the phone Android asks you once, for all of them; on the PC the Recycle Bin). The new version takes the old one's place in your playlists, queue and plays. Needs Tidal; the list goes across with Sync.

## 5.0 · November 2026
- **Find duplicates in every playlist (the phone and the PC):** each playlist has a **Find duplicates** button. It lists the songs that are in the playlist more than once (the same song twice, or two copies of it, like "♡" by Coldplay or "Ruby" next to "11 Ruby"), the copy that plays from this device first. Each copy has **Keep** or **Remove**, and each song **Not a duplicate**. Remove only takes the copy out of the playlist: your files stay. A copy that can't be played any more starts marked Remove.
- **Duplicates sees through track numbers, signs and missing artists (the phone and the PC):** a song named with its track number in front ("11 Ruby", "11. Ruby", "11 - Ruby", "11_Ruby", "(11) Ruby") is now found as a duplicate of "Ruby", and so is a file with no artist in its tags. Names made only of signs ("♡") are found too. Copies found this way are never marked Remove until you choose: play them first.
- **Songs that can't be played don't show any more (the phone and the PC):** each time your music is read, a song in your playlists, queue or favorites whose file is gone from this device is linked to your copy of it (moved, renamed, or named with its track number like "11 Ruby"). With no copy, the same song is found to stream. A song that's on neither is taken out of your playlists, the queue and favorites, and listed in Settings › Problems. Files of your other device (in a synced playlist) are left alone, and if a lot are gone at once (a memory card out, a drive unplugged), nothing is taken out.
- **A playlist holds each song once:** when a song's file moved, or the other device's copy was matched to yours, a playlist could end up with the same song twice (why "only bangers" had more songs than My music). It now keeps one.
- **Lyrics scanner: no timing first, no streamed songs (the phone and the PC):** in Settings › Scanner › Lyrics, **Lyrics but no timing** and its auto-sync button are now at the top, so you don't have to scroll past every song with no lyrics. Streamed songs (not your files or downloads) are no longer counted or listed there: they have no file to put lyrics into, so they sat in "still to find" for ever. Their lyrics are still found when you play them, and are now kept in the app, so they show offline and after a restart.

## 4.5.16 · November 2026
- **Auto-sync missing synced lyrics (the phone and the PC):** after the lyrics scan (Settings › Scanner › Lyrics), the songs that have their words but no timing are listed apart from the ones with no lyrics at all. **Start auto-syncing missing synced lyrics** looks each one up once more, and the ones nobody has timed (your unreleased songs) it listens to: it hears the words being sung and when, on that device, and times the lines itself. The timing goes into the song's file, so Sync takes it to your other device. Songs done stay done and are listed; each start only does the ones left.
