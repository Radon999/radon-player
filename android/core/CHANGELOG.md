# Changelog

What's new in each update of Radon Player, the newest at the top, from 4.0 on. Everything the app does is in **Features**; what the versions before 4.0 changed is kept on GitHub (CHANGELOG.md).

## 4.0.14 · November 2026
- **Thirty more of everything in Personalization:** 30 new looks, 30 new themes, 30 new layouts, 30 new effects, 30 new visualizers, 30 new visualizer colour blends, 30 new Wrapped styles, 30 new lyrics cards, and 30 new presets that put them together. None is a recolour of what was there. The new looks are after materials and places (Stained glass, Copper patina, Blueprint, Terrazzo, Art deco, Bauhaus, Jade, Coral reef, Ink wash, Denim, Velvet theatre, Glacier cave, Origami, Marble hall, Peacock, Lighthouse, Tea house, Crayon box, Lantern festival, Game cartridge, Opal, Kite sky and more), each with an effect drawn for it (panes of glass, drafting lines, deco fans, paper cranes, bunting, lanterns, kites…). The new visualizers are things: organ pipes, an abacus, piano keys, stalactites, pennants, a rose window, a compass, a lotus, an ink circle, a snow crystal, a gem, a pinwheel. Each new preset (Cathedral, Drawing office, Opening night, Tea ceremony, Player two, Windy day…) sets a look, its effect, a new theme and layout, two new visualizers in the look's own blend, and the Wrapped style and lyrics card made to match.
- **Follows the cover, a preset that changes with every song:** the Now playing look already takes its colours from the cover of the song playing. This preset (first in the Presets menu) goes further: each cover picks a whole scene to match, the effects behind everything and Now playing's two visualizers, all drawn in the cover's colour. There are 17 scenes, so one is ready for any cover: twelve by colour round the colour wheel (Embers for red, Lantern light for orange, Fireflies for yellow, Meadow, Forest, Jungle pool, Reef for teal, Open sky, Rain for blue, Galaxy for violet, Dream for purple, Blossom for pink), and Noir, Silver, Mist, Night and Daylight for covers that are black-and-white, grey, very dark or very pale. The whole app's background becomes the cover as if blurred right out behind everything: its own colours running down the window (a cover that is teal above, red in the middle and navy below gives a window that is too), under the Glass theme's see-through cards. It leaves your layout alone, and keeps the effects and visualizers you picked yourself: they come back when you put another preset on or turn off **Scenes from the cover** (Personalization, above Effects), which says which scene is on now.
- **Glass cover, a see-through preset:** with it on, the cover of the song playing is drawn blurred behind the whole app, every screen, washed in the cover's own colour, and everything sits over it see-through: the lists, cards, chips and bars are frosted panes you see the blurred cover through (it puts on the Frosted pills theme). It changes with every song. Your effects, visualizers and layout stay as they are. The switch is **Blurred cover behind everything** (Personalization, above Effects), which works with any theme; a see-through one (Glass, Frosted pills, Cut glass) shows it best.
- **Details for support (Settings › General):** one Copy button for everything someone helping would ask: the version, the device, how many songs and playlists, the look and layout, sync, free space, and what Problems says. No sign-ins, folders or song names are in it.
- **Storage and damaged files (Settings › Scanner › Your files):** one check that looks at every song file and says what takes the space, by kind of file, artist and album, with the biggest songs, as bars. It also lists files that are damaged in a way that shows without playing them: empty, not a music file at all, or far too small for the song's length (cut short while copying or downloading), each with its real name and the reason. Nothing is changed or deleted. A file that is whole but faulty somewhere inside can still only be found by playing it.
- **What's new, after an update:** the first time the app opens after it has been updated, a card names what the update brought, with a button to read about them in the Changelog. Once for each version; never on a fresh install.
- **Favorites sync by themselves too:** Auto playlist sync (Settings › Remote & sync) now carries your favorites as well as your synced playlists: star a song on the phone and it is starred on the PC within about 20 seconds while both apps are open, and the other way.
- **A song as a picture card:** a song's Options has **Share as a picture card**: its cover, name and artist on a card in any of the lyrics card styles, with a line of your own if you like, to save or share as a picture. No link is made.
- **Send an album or a few songs to your other device:** pick songs (or an album's), open Options and choose **Send to the PC** (or the phone): they arrive there as a playlist, named after the album when they are all from one. As with Send a playlist it is a copy made once, and any song the other device can't play is named.
- **The phone edits the PC's queue:** with the phone as the PC's remote, the queue on the phone can now be reordered by dragging, cleared, and undone, as well as songs removed and added as before. Both need this version.
- **What a sync changed:** under Sync in Settings, **What the last sync changed here** lists it in words: which playlists gained or lost songs (or are new, renamed or gone), how many favorites came and went, and which other things were brought up to date.
- **On this day (Most played):** under Milestones, what you were listening to this week a year ago, two years ago and further back, from your listening history, each year with a Play button.
- **Presets by time of day (Settings › Personalization):** pick a preset for the morning, the day, the evening and the night, and each is put on by itself when that part of the day begins (and when the app opens in it). A part left on No change keeps whatever is on.
- **Back up by itself (Settings › Backup):** every day or every week the app saves a backup of its own accord while it is open, and keeps the newest five. Save a copy puts the newest wherever you choose; on the phone that matters, since the app's own folder goes when the app is uninstalled.
- **Song radio:** a song's Options has **Song radio (songs like this)**: the song plays, and after it songs like it from your streaming service, as a queue of its own.
- **Running out of room:** Settings › Your music says how much space is free where your downloads go, and a warning comes up when you start a download with under 1 GB (or 3%) left.
- **See it before you put it on:** every Personalization menu shows each choice as several live screens to swipe through, not one picture. A preset shows Now playing with its visualizers moving, My music and Albums in its look, theme and layout with its effects behind them, three slides of Wrapped (top songs, top artist, your year) and its lyrics card. A look shows Now playing, My music, Albums and a Wrapped slide; a theme and a layout show the lists they change; an effect shows by itself, behind Now playing and behind My music, moving; a visualizer shows on Now playing and by itself; a Wrapped style shows three slides. The songs in them are samples.
- **The Personalization menus fold into sections:** with this much to pick from, each menu now opens as a short list of sections, your ★ favourites first, with only the section of the one in use open: Looks by Basics, Light, Dark, Neon and Materials and places; Themes by what they change; Layouts by where the buttons are; Effects by Sky, Nature, Patterns, Light and Things; visualizers by where they stand. Everything is still there, a tap further in.
- **Undo for the queue:** cleared the queue, shuffled it, dragged a song to the wrong place or removed one by accident? An **Undo** button shows at the top of the queue (Up next, and Now playing's queue) and puts it back as it was, saying what it undoes ("Undo: shuffled"). The last ten changes are kept, so you can step back through them. The song that's playing is never interrupted. Starting something new (a song tapped, another queue) isn't a change to undo.
- **Changes waiting to sync:** a synced playlist you have changed since the last sync says **⇄ SYNCED · CHANGES WAITING** in every list of playlists, so you can see what the other device hasn't had yet. It goes back to ⇄ SYNCED after the next Sync (or by itself with Auto playlist sync). It is only said while a phone and PC are paired.
- **Tidied inside:** 28 pieces of code nothing used any more were taken out. Nothing you see changes.

## 4.0.13 · November 2026
- **Moods into files that kept refusing them (phone):** writing moods could end with "48 couldn't be written: its tag is of a kind the app leaves alone" and no more. Three kinds of file were being turned away without a try: WAV files, MP3s whose tag the app's own writer can't read, and songs downloaded as Opus or M4A (from YouTube Music). They are now written through the tag library, in their own format, as your other files are. And when a file still can't take a mood, the message says what kind of file it is and what stood in the way (All files access not allowed, the file not where the phone says, the phone not letting the app change it), with the files' real names in Settings › Problems instead of numbers.

## 4.0.12 · November 2026
- **Playlists that sync (Settings › Remote & sync):** one list of all your playlists, each with a switch. The ones switched on are kept the same on your phone and PC; the rest stay on the device they are on. Nothing is sent when you flip a switch: Sync now, in the same section, sends them. **Listen later** and the **discovery playlists** can be switched on there too. A synced playlist's songs come together: what you add or take out on the phone is on the PC's, and the other way. Playlists each device made by itself are joined too: two synced playlists of the same name become one with the songs of both, each song once; so do the two Listen laters, and the two discovery playlists for an artist (a song heard to the end on either device is off the list to hear). Nothing is lost by joining. Smart playlists can sync too: the rules go across, and each device fills the playlist from its own music. **Switch them all on** sets every playlist you have to sync in one tap. Queues don't sync. Both devices need this version.
- **Send a playlist to your other device (Settings › Remote & sync, or a playlist's menu › Send a copy):** copies one playlist to the phone or the PC, once. It isn't a sync: the two go their own ways afterwards. The other device adds the songs it can play (the ones that stream, and its own copy of each of your files); a song that is only a file on the sending device is left out and named, and Settings keeps the list of what was left out. Listen later lands on the other's Listen later, a discovery playlist in its discovery playlist for that artist, and a playlist whose name is there already gets only the songs it hasn't. From the phone it goes at once; from the PC the phone takes it within 20 seconds when its app is open on the same network.
- **Auto playlist sync (Settings › Remote & sync):** a switch, off until you turn it on. With it on, your synced playlists sync by themselves: the moment the phone's app finds the PC's (both open, on the same network), and again within about 20 seconds whenever a song is added or taken out on either while both are open. Only the playlists go across this way; plays, favorites, settings and the rest still wait for Sync now. Turning it on on either device is enough. It can't sync while one app is closed or the two are on different networks: they catch up the next time they meet.
- **Sync says plainly that it waits for you:** a synced playlist is the same on both devices after a Sync, not as you go. Without Auto playlist sync, songs you add or take out cross only when you tap Sync now. Settings says so in full view under Playlists that sync, and so does the message when you switch a playlist on.
- **New playlists are set to sync:** any playlist you make from now on (normal, smart, a discovery playlist, Listen later) is set to sync, since that is what you nearly always want. **Settings › Your files › New playlists sync** turns that off; one playlist can be kept to its device with its switch, or made with **New playlist for this device only**. The playlists you had before aren't changed: use Switch them all on.
- **You can see which playlists sync:** every list of playlists (Playlists, Smart playlists, Discovery, Listen later, Add to playlist) marks each one **⇄ SYNCED**, or **THIS DEVICE ONLY** in grey.

## 4.0.11 · November 2026
- **Other device's songs (Settings › Remote & sync):** keeps your phone's and your PC's music the same without moving files across or downloading everything twice. At each Sync the two tell each other every song they have (names only: no files cross). Each then lists the songs the other has that it hasn't, as a checklist: tick the ones you want, and Options › Download gets them from your streaming service into My music; Options › Leave out takes the rest off the list for good (Bring back undoes that). Songs are matched as Most played matches them, however each device's files are tagged. Nothing is downloaded by itself. Both devices need this version.
- **Every menu entry can go on your bar:** making a layout of your own, the bar's buttons offered only some of the app's screens (Discovery, for one, was nowhere to pick). Everything in the ☰ menu can be put on the bar now: Discovery, Listen later, Downloads, EPs, Singles, Folders, Smart playlists, Mood lists, New releases, Pinned, Skips, the FLAC tabs, Lyrics and the rest.
- **Why a mood couldn't be written:** writing moods into the files could end with "48 couldn't be written" and nothing more. It now says why (the kind of file, or what the phone answered), and Settings › Problems lists the reasons with how many files each, and a few of the files by name.

## 4.0.10 · November 2026
- **A playlist sorts by what its songs are now:** a playlist keeps each song as it was when you put it in. A song you later moved from a single into its album or EP (or gave a tidier artist name) was still sorted under the old one, so sorted by album it sat apart from the album it belongs to. Sorting now goes by each song's tags as they are today.
- **A smart playlist of all your music:** the rule "Downloaded or my files" could only say "is downloaded" or "is one of my files", so a playlist meant to hold your whole library, made from "is one of my files", left out every song downloaded in the app. It has a third choice now, **is either (all my music)**: every song you have, however it got there. A live playlist with it grows as you download.
- **Refresh your music folders (Settings › Your music):** a Refresh button reads your folders again when you ask. They are also read again by themselves when downloads finish: after one download, or once the last of a big batch is in.
- **The song count in Settings adds up:** Folders said "3,280 songs found" while the folder chooser showed 3,322 for the same folder. Both were right: your downloads are files in that folder too, but the app keeps them as downloads, apart from the files it finds. Settings now says every song in My music and how it divides (files found, and downloads).
- **Coming back to an artist's page is lighter still:** your songs and albums of theirs are kept with the rest of the page while your music hasn't changed, instead of being gathered from all your songs on every return.

## 4.0.9 · November 2026
- **Back is smooth:** coming back from an album or a song to an artist's page or a search no longer stutters. A list that is in memory now opens straight at the place you were (it used to open at the top, then jump), and an artist's page keeps what it found for half an hour, so it shows at once on coming back and nothing is looked up again.
- **Write moods into the files is always on the Moods tab:** the button used to show only when it had something to write, so it was often simply not there. It is always there now, with Prevent auto mood scans on or off. It writes the moods the app has worked out into the files that have none, and never touches a file that has moods already: those change only when you clear them with the buttons for that and scan again. With nothing to write, tapping it says why (not scanned yet, or files the app isn't allowed to change). Beside it, **Overwrite the moods that differ** counts the files that hold other moods than the app has worked out since, and replaces those when you ask (it asks first). The tab also says separately how many files are empty and how many hold other moods than the app worked out.
- **The Moods tab opens without holding the app still:** all its counting (every mood's songs, what is scanned, what is left to write) was done on the screen's own thread as the tab opened. It is done beside it now, and kept, so the tab opens at once after the first time and is counted again only when your music or its moods change.
- **A censored title is the same song:** your copy named "Just Don't Give a F**k" and the service's "Just Don't Give A Fuck" are one song, but as names they differ, so your copy was taken for a song you didn't have: it showed among the streaming songs and offered to download again. When songs are compared, a censored word is now read as the word it stands for (stars, other signs or a gap standing in for the letters left out). Titles are always shown as they are written, and ordinary names with signs in them (P!nk, N.P.D) are left alone.
- **An artist's page folds, like Settings:** its sections (Albums and singles, Featured on, Every song, Your songs, Top songs, Left out of this page) are rows that fold open and shut, each saying how many it holds, and all are shut when the page opens: you see what there is and open the one you want, with no scrolling past the others. What's left out is a section like the rest, not a button at the very end. Which ones you opened is kept while you go into an album and come back.
- **Listen later:** a new tab by Discovery (☰ menu), for music you want to hear but can't right now. Pick songs, albums or EPs anywhere, open Options and choose **Add to Listen later playlist**; they wait on the tab, the last added first, with Play, Shuffle and the usual Sort. Options on the tab has **Remove from Listen later**. Unlike a discovery playlist it holds only what you put on it. It is kept as a playlist of yours named Listen later, so it is with your other playlists too.
- **Hide on an artist's page works, and at once:** picking releases far down an artist's page and tapping Hide did nothing: the bar's options were made by a row at the top of the list, which isn't there once you've scrolled past it, so they were for nothing ticked. They now come from the page itself and act on what is ticked when you tap. What you hide disappears as you hide it, with no need to leave the page and come back, and a message says how many were hidden.
- **Album cover's colour, for the visualizer:** a new colour in every visualizer colour menu (the Visualizer's, Now playing's, a style of your own). It follows the cover of what's playing, whatever look you have on: any look with a visualizer in the cover's colour. A cover too dark to see is lifted, its hue kept. The other way round was there already: the Now playing look puts the whole app in the cover's colours, and the visualizer can then take any colour you pick.
- **Leave things out of Personalization Preset Shuffle:** under the shuffle's switch, each kind of choice a preset holds (the look, the theme, the layout, the effects, the Now playing visual, the visualizer, its colour, the Wrapped style, the lyrics card) can be left out. What you leave out stays as you set it while the shuffle changes the rest: shuffle the visualizers and keep your look, say. Picking a preset yourself still puts on all of it.

## 4.0.8 · November 2026
- **A song that goes silent partway starts again by itself (phone):** one 24-bit song played with sound for about twenty seconds, then went on counting in silence until it was moved. Moving it brought the sound back, so the app now does that itself: when music stops coming out though the song is playing, it starts the song again from the same place within a few seconds, and Settings › Problems says where the sound stopped.
- **Featured on, on an artist's page:** between their albums and singles and Every song, a list of other artists' songs that feature them, so the page has everything they made and not only their own discography. It is a list of songs: one feature never brings in the other artist's whole album; a release is shown only when every song on it features them. A song they lead, with or without guests, stays in their own list. Found from the releases they appear on, the service's search for their name, their top songs and (with a Genius artist picked) Genius's list. A feature can be hidden like any other song.
- **Your songs on an artist's page, as songs or as releases:** a dropdown by its Sort menu shows your songs of theirs as a list of songs, as before, or as your albums, EPs and singles of theirs, so with a big discography you tap the album instead of scrolling to it. Your choice is kept, and the one Sort menu applies to both: the releases follow the same sort as the songs (most played is the release whose songs you played most).
- **Plays on the rows when sorted by plays:** on an artist's page, with Your songs or Every song sorted by Most or Least played, each song's row says how often you played it (your releases too, added up).
- **Back on Now playing goes back to where you were:** from a search, an artist's page, a playlist or any other list, going to Now playing and pressing Back now returns you to that list as you left it (the page, your place's own search). Before, Back opened the menu and the list was gone. When you weren't anywhere before (the app opened on Now playing), Back opens the menu as it did.
- **Back to where you were, on every list:** opening something from far down a list (an album from an artist's page, an artist from a playlist, a search result) and pressing Back returned you to the top of that list. Every screen's list now keeps its place: Back returns you to where you had scrolled to. Coming back to a tab later finds it where you left it too; a page opened afresh starts at its top.
- **Local or streaming, on an artist's releases:** with Everything showing, each album, EP and single says under its year whether it is yours (LOCAL: your files or downloads) or plays from a service (STREAMING), marked the way a song's FLAC is. A release you have only part of says BOTH, with how many of its songs are local and how many are streaming.
- **Your songs on an artist's page are only yours:** the list also held songs of theirs you don't have (ones the app knew from your listening history or a service's library). It now lists only what is on the device: your files and downloads. The song count at the top of the page counts the same.
- **The Tagger skips streaming songs, and says so:** tagging a selection that has streaming songs in it (an album you have part of, picked on an artist's page) tags only the songs you have, and a message says how many streaming songs were skipped and that they have to be downloaded to be tagged.
- **Pick several releases on an artist's page:** hold an album, EP or single (or tap Select by the Sort menu) and tick as many as you like. The bar at the bottom pins them all, hides them all from the page (or puts hidden ones back), and its Options has everything a song's Options has, for all their songs. Songs on the page are picked as anywhere else, and their Options has Hide from this artist's page.

## 4.0.7 · November 2026
- **An artist's whole discography:** a release could be missing from an artist's page (an older EP, say). YouTube Music's artist page shows only the first few of each kind, and the app never asked for the rest: it does now. And the releases Genius lists for the artist that the service still leaves out are added, marked "on Genius"; open one and its songs play from your copy, or are looked for on Tidal and YouTube.
- **Singles that are on an album or EP already are folded away (streaming only):** on an artist's page a service's single whose song is on one of their albums or EPs is left out, so the song is found on its release and the list is shorter. In a service's search, a song found both as its single and on a release shows as the release's, and the single is not listed among the albums. An EP always stays, also when an album has its songs. Your own files are never touched by this: a single you have stays in your music. What was left out is under Show what's left out, and can be put back.
- **Every song of an artist shows by itself:** the Show every song button is gone. Every song is listed right under their albums and singles as soon as the page has gone through their releases, above your songs and their top songs. With Only my music picked, it shows only what you have, as before.
- **Only streaming, on an artist's page:** a third choice in the menu at the top, beside Everything and Only my music: what the services have, without your own files.
- **Sort an artist's page as the other tabs:** a Sort menu for their releases (newest, oldest, by name), for your songs (title, album, added, most and least played, last played, length, shuffled) and for Every song. Each is kept.
- **Genius search finds artists and releases too:** it used to list only songs. It now shows artists, then albums, EPs and singles, and a song by itself only when it was released by itself: a song on an album or EP shows as that release, which opens with all its songs. Tapping one of several same-named artists opens that name's page sorted with the one you tapped.
- **A song that went silent partway (phone):** the sound could stop a little way into a song while the song went on counting. If the equalizer's numbers ever run away (a band at or over half the song's sample rate could make them), they are set back at once and the music goes on, and a band can no longer be built that high. The same for the player's own volume: a crossfade or the DJ turns it down and brings it back, and if one was ever cut short nothing brought it back; a volume left down with nothing fading is now put back within a few seconds (on the computer too). If it happens again, Settings › Problems now says exactly what turned the sound down.
- **Write moods into the files is back on the Moods tab:** with Prevent auto mood scans on, the tab said songs were waiting to have their moods written but had no button for it. The button is there now, also with that switch on (the switch stops what the app does by itself, not what you ask for), and beside Scan moods when songs are still to be scanned. It only fills in files that have no moods in them yet (a file that has moods keeps them), from songs already scanned, and scans nothing.
- **Picking a song in the queue picks that one only:** with the same song waiting in the queue more than once, holding one of them picked every one. Each row of the queue is now its own: pick one and only it is ticked, and Remove from the queue takes out just the rows you picked.

## 4.0.6 · November 2026
- **Hold a song and picking starts:** holding a song (right-click on a computer) starts multi-select at once with that song picked, everywhere: lists, grids and the queue. Before, some places opened the song's menu and Select was at the bottom of it. A song's own menu is its ⋮; while picking, holding a song opens the options for what's picked.
- **Shuffle shuffles the whole list:** started halfway down a playlist, Shuffle used to mix only what was still to come, so the songs above never played. It now takes every song of the list, with the one playing first and the ones you queued next.
- **Move, don't duplicate (Settings › Playback):** a switch for what Play next and Add to queue do with a song already waiting in the queue. Off, as it always was: it's put in again. On: it's moved to where you put it, so it's in the queue once.
- **The app's messages get out of the way:** tap one, or swipe it to either side, and it's gone, so it never keeps you from Play, Pause or Skip.
- **An artist's page starts with their albums and singles,** then your songs, then their top songs; and your songs are sorted by album until you pick another sort (which is then kept).
- **Every song of an artist:** at the bottom of an artist's page, Show every song goes through all their releases (albums, EPs, singles, and the compilations they are on) and lists each song once. Their top songs are only the service's twenty most played, so a song that is not one of those was nowhere on the page.
- **Two bands with the same name:** when a service has more than one artist under a name, the page offers which one, each with a song it is known for. Your choice is kept, so the name means that band from then on, and the page shows only its songs and albums from the services. Your own files carry only the name, so those still show together.
- **A mixed-up artist sorted with Genius:** some services put two bands of one name under a single artist, songs and albums all mixed (it happens to small, little-known bands). When Genius has more than one artist of that name, the page offers **Sort with Genius**: pick the one you mean, and only the songs and releases on that artist's list on Genius stay. It is a separate switch from the service's own "which one?": use either, or both, or turn it off. Show every song then also lists the songs only Genius knows, each once (Genius's separate pages for the album's, the single's and the EP's version of a song count as one).
- **Hide songs and albums from an artist's page yourself:** for when both the service and Genius get it wrong. A song's Options (or an album's menu) has "Hide from this artist's page"; pick several songs first to hide them together. Nothing is deleted: **Show what's left out** at the end of the page lists everything hidden or left out by Genius, and "Show on this artist's page" puts one back, whatever Genius says. Your own files are never left out unless you hide them.
- **Search Genius:** a new place in the search bar's menu. Genius lists nearly every song, so one that no service has can still be found by name. It has no music itself: a song you have plays from your copy, any other is looked for on Tidal and YouTube when you play it.
- **Streamed songs have their covers in Android Auto:** the cover of a song that streams was handed to the car in a way it could not open, so only downloaded songs and your own files showed one. It is also fetched before the song starts now, so it is there the first time.
- **The music no longer stops when the sound moves between your earbuds and the car (phone):** with both connected, Android tells the app the same thing as when headphones are unplugged, and the song paused partway. It still pauses for a moment, then goes on by itself when something is still connected to play through. Unplugging your only headphones pauses as before.

## 4.0.5 · November 2026
- **Checking for an update always finishes:** Settings could stay on "checking for a newer version…" for good. The check waited its turn among the app's other background work (all of it busy during a scan or a sync) and had no time limit of its own. It now runs by itself, ends within about a minute whatever the network does, and has a second way to find the newest version when GitHub won't give its list. Downloading the update no longer waits for other work either.

## 4.0.4 · November 2026
- **A song you have is never downloaded twice:** if you already have a song with the same title and artist (whatever its album is tagged as), Download leaves it and says so: your copy is the one that plays. Before, a download whose tags you changed (a single moved into its album) could be taken for a song you didn't have, streamed instead of played from your file, and downloaded again as a second copy. "Keep both" (Settings, or when asked about a near match) still downloads it.

## 4.0.3 · November 2026
