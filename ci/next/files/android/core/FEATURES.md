# Radon Player

**All your music in one place, on your phone and your PC.**

Your own music files and your streaming services in one app, in FLAC (hi-res where it's available). The Android and Windows apps are one app built from the same code, so they look and work the same, and they can pair and sync.

**Works best with:** a Tidal subscription plus your own song files.

---

## Playing music

- **Now playing:** A big cover with ☆ favorite and ⋯ options. Swipe the cover to skip, swipe up for the queue, tap for lyrics, double-tap to pause.
- **Quality badge:** Shows what you're hearing ("FLAC 24-bit/96 kHz", a stream's quality or "Offline"). **⤓ FLAC** saves a stream.
- **Queue:** Drag to reorder. **Queues** can keep a separate queue for each album, playlist and folder.
- **Shuffle, Repeat and Autoplay:** Smart shuffle favors songs you love but haven't heard lately. Autoplay keeps similar songs coming.
- **Per song:** Its own volume, EQ and speed.
- **Mono** for one earbud, and **casting** to Chromecast, Google speakers and TVs.
- **Your copy first:** Wherever a song shows up (playlists, search, radios, the DJ), your own file plays when you have it.

## Your library

Everything is in the ☰ menu. Every page can be a list, grid or compact grid, with its own search and sort.

- **My music, Albums, EPs, Singles, Artists, Genres and Folders.** Albums show **Missing from this album** to download the rest.
- **Artist pages:** Your songs, a new picture, a **Discovery playlist**, an artist playlist and **Merge into…**.
- **Playlists:** Make, sort, fold into folders, import and export (.m3u8).
- **Smart playlists:** Fill themselves by rules (genre, mood, plays, format, skips and more), with ready-made ones.
- **More lists:** Favorites, Recently played, Downloads, 24-bit and 32-bit FLACs, Skips, and Pinned items.
- **Select:** Hold down on a song (right-click on the PC) to pick several songs, albums or playlists and act on them all.
- **Search:** Ignores symbols ("acdc" finds AC/DC), with voice typing.
- **Covers everywhere**, and **moved files followed**: rename a folder and playlists still find the songs.
- **Nothing deleted without asking.**

## Streaming & downloads

- **Tidal:** Streams and downloads in FLAC; its favorites, playlists and radios work as a library.
- **Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer, YouTube Music:** Sign in to bring your library and playlists over. Their songs play from Tidal (or YouTube when Tidal doesn't have one).
- **SoundCloud:** Streaming only (sign in with your own SoundCloud app). Search, play your likes and playlists, artists and radio. Its songs can't be downloaded or put in your playlists, and a SoundCloud queue holds only SoundCloud songs.
- **Search any of them** from the menu by the search bar, or paste a link.
- **Downloads:** From Tidal with full tags, cover and genre, into Music › Artists › the artist › the release. Whole playlists too, skipping songs you have.
- **Quality:** FLAC 24-bit, FLAC 16-bit or 320 kbps, for streaming and downloads separately.
- **New releases:** Checks every artist you have for new albums, EPs and singles.

## Moods

Every song is sorted into 12 moods: Happy, Energetic, Party, Hype, Chill, Calm, Dreamy, Focus, Romantic, Sad, Dark and Intense. A song can have several.

- **How:** The app listens to each song (**Scan moods**, or the surer **Full scan**) and reads its lyrics for what they mean, not single words.
- **Time estimates:** Each scan says about how long it will take before you start, and shows the time left beside its progress.
- **AI reading (optional):** Use your own Anthropic API key and Claude reads the lyrics instead. **Test the key** always tells you whether it worked.
- **Fix one:** Tagger › Tags › Mood. Similar songs learn from your fix. The Moods tab lists songs it's unsure of to check.
- **Saved in your files:** Every mood goes in the file's MOOD tag, and Sync and backups carry them all, so a scan is done once.
- **Rescan moods:** Only for when moods were lost or spoilt.
- **Used in:** Mood lists, Smart playlists, the DJ, Most played and Wrapped.

## The DJ

Short sets from *your* listening, with a voice between them, like a radio host.

- **Sets:** On repeat, this time of day, favorites, throwbacks, forgotten loves, moods, genres, and new music like yours. Songs don't repeat for 20 sets.
- **The voice:** Says what's coming and fun facts about your listening. On the PC a natural voice; on the phone Google's voice, or download the natural one (about 130 MB) and pick either in DJ › Voice settings (or remove it).
- **Ask for something:** Type or say an artist, genre, decade, mood or vibe.
- **Play from** everything, or one playlist, folder or album.

## Stats & Wrapped

- **Your plays:** From Last.fm and imported histories (Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer, YouTube Music, Tidal), never counted twice.
- **Most played:** Top songs, albums, artists and moods for any time, with charts of when you listen. Plus Repeat, Milestones and Skips.
- **Tidy stats:** Merge an artist or song counted two ways.
- **Wrapped:** Your week, month, year or all time as slides: top 15s, minutes, streaks, discoveries, listener type and a summary card. Singles and EPs are checked online.
- **Save and share** any slide, share card, collage or lyrics card.

## Lyrics

- **Synced** (line by line, tap to jump) or **Genius** (with annotations).
- **Lyrics card:** Up to 15 lines on a picture with the cover, in the colours of the look you're using.
- **Lyrics scanner:** Finds and writes lyrics into every file at once.

## Sound

- **Equalizer:** One per output (speaker, headphones, each Bluetooth device, USB DAC, car), 3 to 31 bands, presets, and **Auto EQ** headphone correction.
- **Boost, Crossfeed, Crossfade** and smart transitions.
- **Sound quality:** Clip guard, smoothing of harsh loud masters, dither, high-quality resampling, loudness compensation and detail.
- **Bit-perfect** to a USB DAC or the PC's sound card, and **Ignore Bluetooth devices**.

## Personalization

Each device keeps its own look, theme, layout and visualizer; Sync shares the ones you made.

- **Looks:** Light, Dark, Now playing (every screen in the cover's colours), dozens of themes and neon looks.
- **Themes:** How the app is drawn (Rounded, Sharp, Glass, Retro…), with any look.
- **Layouts:** 15 ways round: ☰ menu, bottom bar, side rail or top tabs, mini player styles, and 6 Now playing arrangements.
- **Previews:** Every menu shows a picture of each choice (point at it on the PC, tap it on the phone).
- **Favorites, Edit, Delete:** Hold down on a look, theme, layout, effect or visualizer (right-click on the PC): ★ Favorite puts it at the top; your own can also be edited or deleted there.
- **Effects:** 31 effects (stars, aurora, rain, snow, synthwave grid, nebula…). Your own looks keep theirs; the ready-made looks can take any from Settings. Each can have its own colour. Rain and snow fall over the buttons too.

### Make your own

- **Look:** Your colours, a fade, neon glow, stars and effects, shown live.
- **Layout:** The bar's buttons, the mini player, Now playing and many finer choices, with a big live preview.
- **Visualizer:** A style shaped your way (mirror, flip, spin, glow, strength), or a colour blend. **Dual** plays a middle style and a bottom style at once. **Full screen on Now playing** shows it over the whole screen. Test it with any song.
- **Randomize** in every maker, and **Randomize colours** for just the colours.
- **Saved colours:** Save colour sets and pick from them, with 15 ready-made sets in each.
- **Yours everywhere:** What you make goes to your other device at each Sync, and into backups.

## Visualizer

- **30 styles** (bars, waves, rings, starfield, galaxy, tunnel…) and dozens of colour blends.
- **On Now playing:** Slim bars, any style, or yours, with its own strength. It hears only the app's sound.

## Tags & tidying

- **Tagger:** Tags (with Find tags online), cover, info, lyrics, mood, and which tab a release goes in. Files move to match new tags. On the phone it works on any of your files once you allow All files access.
- **Scanner tools:** Fix genre names, Duplicates, Upgrade to FLAC, Songs without covers, Identify by sound, Similar artists.
- **PC only:** Fix albums and artists, empty folders, and folders to leave alone.

## Phone + PC together

- **Phone remote:** Pair with a code and control the PC from the phone.
- **Sync now:** Plays, favorites, moods, EQs, downloads, tag changes and synced playlists (⇄), with Undo. Each device keeps its own look, theme and layout; the looks, layouts and visualizers you made are added to both.
- **Tags & covers match** from a main device, with folders sync leaves alone.
- **Continue where you left off**, and **back up and restore** with one file.

## Phone only

- **Android Auto** with covers and its own EQ.
- **Lock screen player, notification and widget**, picture-in-picture mini player, and auto mono for chosen earbuds.

## PC only

- **System tray, media keys, Start with Windows** and an always-on-top mini player.
- **Discord** shows what you're playing.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** and right-click menus everywhere.

## Help

- **Updates:** Checked at every start (it says whether there's one); Update installs it.
- **Changelog and Features** in the ☰ menu.
- **Problems:** Settings shows recent errors with a Copy button.
