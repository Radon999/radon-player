# Changelog

Radon Player started as **Omarchy Music** (later called VarMusic), made by Xavier Reid (Varixxl).
Radon999 forked it in 1.1.x and carries it on here as Radon Player. The full history of both is kept
in this repository, so `git log` shows where every feature came from.

## 5.2.11: Plays by album, every edition as one, your queues on top, and Least skipped (Android, Windows and Linux)
- **A song's plays are its album's (the phone and the PC):** a play counts for a song only when its title, its artist and its album are the song's, from every service (Last.fm, Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer, YouTube Music, Tidal). Imagine Dragons' two "Yesterday"s each have their own plays; so do a song's single, its album's copy and a compilation's. A play that came with no album goes to the album played most. **Plays on singles and albums** (Settings) still puts a single's or an EP's plays on the album you pick.
- **Every edition of an album is the one album:** Deluxe, Anniversary, Expanded, Super Deluxe, Remastered…, however it's written ("Night Visions (Deluxe)", "Night Visions: 10th Anniversary Edition", "Night Visions Deluxe Edition"), so a new edition keeps every play of the old one, in your songs' plays and in Top albums. "Night Visions Live" stays its own album.
- **The right copy of a song of one name:** your copy is found on the album asked for (its editions, and a name written a little another way, too), so the other "Yesterday" no longer takes the sessions' one's place in your lists, with its plays.
- **Your queues at the top of Now playing's queue (the phone and the PC):** above the songs, as a dropdown that folds to one line until you open it (it stays as you leave it). They were after the last song.
- **↑ Top and ↓ Back, put right:** where you were is kept for its own page only (another tab starts with none), and after ↑ Top, ↓ Back always shows: a ↓ Back left from another tab had taken you down a list you'd never been down, and then ↑ Top lost its place.
- **Wrapped's Least skipped (the phone and the PC):** in place of Never skipped: the songs you hardly ever skip, the never-skipped ones first, then the ones skipped only now and then, each with how much of its plays you heard to the end. A song needs three plays heard out to be in it.

## 5.2.10: ↑ Top and ↓ Back on every list, the whole Most played, and the Visualizer like Now playing (Android, Windows and Linux)

A layout's Queue button, a shortcut or a link to Up next opens Now playing's queue.

- **Wrapped's songs don't shake the slide any more (the phone and the PC):** a tap or a left click on a song in Wrapped does nothing now (it used to light the row and make the slide shake). Hold it on the phone, or right-click it on the PC, for its menu, as before.
- **Every play counted by its album (the phone and the PC):** where you have one song on more than one album (two different songs of one name, like Imagine Dragons' two "Yesterday"s, or the same song on an album and a compilation), each album has its own plays, every play counted for the album it was on. An album's editions (Deluxe, Remastered…) are one, and a single named after the song goes with it. A play with no album goes to the one you've played most. **Plays on singles and albums** (Settings) still puts a single's or an EP's plays on the album you pick.
- **★ Favorites in the New releases widget (the phone and the PC):** on the PC it's beside All, Albums, EPs and Singles (on their own row now, not off the end of a row that scrolled); on the phone, a ☆ Favorites switch at its top. Either way it goes with the week, month or year picked.
- **Wrapped's Most searched counts again (the phone and the PC):** every play of a song you took from search, played there or put in the queue from it, however it got into the queue. It only counted a song started from search 4 times in one week, so it mostly showed nothing.
- **Wrapped's numbers checked over (the phone and the PC):** On repeat one's time uses each song's real length (292 repeats were "7 minutes": songs found in your Last.fm history had no length); Top albums shows albums only, not singles and EPs; the hours you repeat songs end with the period (an October's took in November too); "vs the month before" compares with that month itself; All time's minutes a day and scrobbles a year are over the days since your first play (a history begun last December had been two years); All time counts every scrobble, from before the year you joined Last.fm too; a song you played before under another name isn't "new to you"; Apple Music's day totals are on your day and spread over it (not all at midnight UTC); minutes use your own files' lengths; songs, milestones and the top artist's song you hid stay hidden; the top song's "first played" says "first this month" outside All time.
- **No more Singer or Songwriter genres (the phone and the PC):** "Singer/Songwriter" in a song's genre tag isn't shown or counted as a genre anywhere (the Genres page, top genres, Wrapped, the DJ). The tag in the file is left as it is.
- **Statistics checked over, every source counted (the phone and the PC):** a song of yours credited to more than one artist ("A & B") gets its Last.fm and imported plays when sorting by plays, in smart playlists and in Unplayed and neglected (it had only the plays here); milestones are dated by the very plays they count (a merged song's or artist's had come out undated, or dated today); Unplayed and neglected knows when a song counted under your file's name was last played; On repeat counts as Most played does (your merges, one row a song, nothing you hid); Repeats' heading counts every song (it stopped at 200); a year's artists and moods count every song (they stopped at 5,000 songs); an artist you hid stays out of Most played and Milestones; the Taste tab's skips are the Skips page's; and **Apple Music's skips** (from its Play Activity file: import it again to bring them) are on the Skips page beside Spotify's. Last.fm, Deezer, YouTube Music and Tidal keep no skips.

## 5.1.2: Easier on the battery (Android)

## 5.1: Queued clean/explicit changes (Android, Windows and Linux)
- **Queued clean/explicit changes (Utilities, the phone and the PC):** songs got from another service or app (DumpMedia mostly) often come as the clean version when you wanted explicit, or the other way round. Select them anywhere and pick **Queue a clean/explicit change**: they go on a list in Utilities › Queued clean/explicit changes. The new version takes the old one's place in your playlists, queue and plays. Needs Tidal; the list goes across with Sync.
